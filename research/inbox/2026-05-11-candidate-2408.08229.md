# Candidate Paper Card

**Title:** Quantum Gravitational Corrections in Cosmology

**arXiv:** 2408.08229

**Date:** 2024-08-15 (v1), 2025-01-04 (v2)

**Authors:** Marco Sebastianutti

**Source:** arXiv

**Claims:**
- Reliable calculation of quantum gravitational corrections to cosmological models
- Uses unique effective action formalism for quantum gravity
- Model independent approach applicable to any UV-complete QG theory admitting GR as low-energy limit
- Full effective action needed for renormalization group invariant solutions
- Validity range investigated within simple cosmological models

**Evidence Strength:** high

**Tags:** quantum-gravity, cosmology, effective-action, corrections

**Summary:** Presents model-independent method for calculating quantum gravitational corrections in cosmology using effective action formalism, showing importance of full effective action for RG-invariant solutions.
