## Executive verdict

I read the archive as a set of competing **OpenQG unified-theory candidate dossiers**, not as normal physics essays. The brief is trying to force an agent to separate three things that are usually blurred:

1. **Real derivations** that can emit strict, fixture-independent prediction rows.
2. **Structural unification arguments** that are meaningful but do not yet predict numeric fixture values.
3. **Unsupported empirical/catalog/state rows** that must be marked `not_implemented`, not filled with copied constants, fitted parameters, or benchmark leakage.

The best final answer is **not** “claim all rows are solved.” The best answer is a hardened, auditable candidate that derives the Standard Model’s structural core, recovers GR/QFT limits, emits only three strict row families today, and creates a disciplined path for future expansion.

## What all attempts are still missing

The hard missing pieces are the same across nearly every file:

**1. No strict derivation of dimensionless constants.**
None of the attempts derives (\alpha), (g_1,g_2,g_3), (v/M_{\rm Pl}), (m_H), (\Lambda_{\rm QCD}/M_{\rm Pl}), (m_p/m_e), or the Yukawa spectrum from first principles.

**2. No solved flavor sector.**
They write the formal seesaw/Yukawa/CKM/PMNS machinery, but none derives the actual matrices fixture-independently. This matters because current neutrino fits remain empirical global analyses; NuFIT 6.0, revised in 2025, reports current oscillation constraints and remaining ordering/CP/octant ambiguities rather than a first-principles derivation. ([arXiv][11])

**3. No solved cosmological state selection.**
The attempts can write FLRW, BAO, CMB, Pantheon, and inflation equations, but they do not derive (H_0,\Omega_m,\Omega_\Lambda,A_s,n_s,\eta_B,\Omega_{\rm DM}), or the vacuum energy. DESI BAO constraints, for example, are robust empirical measurements of distances and expansion relative to the sound horizon; they are not outputs of these candidate theories unless the candidate derives the cosmological state without using target rows. ([Astrophysics Data System][12])

**4. No source-state derivation for catalog observables.**
GWOSC event masses, EHT angular sizes, JPL orbital elements, SPARC rotation curves, and Pantheon distance moduli require boundary conditions, source histories, baryonic profiles, object masses/distances, or initial conditions. A law can say how to compute them from state data; it does not automatically derive each catalog row.

**5. The generation-count claim is too weak.**
Several attempts claim a rank/index/topological rule for exactly three generations. This is promising but not yet rigorous enough to count as a completed derivation. It should be listed as **partial structural support**, not a strict numeric prediction.

**6. No adapter was actually run.**
The previous answers are dossiers, not executed OpenQG runs. Therefore the only honest score above 85 is the **pre-submission dossier score**, not official benchmark performance.
