# OpenQG — architecture, methods, data, and open problems

> A self-contained technical brief for an outside physicist asked to review this project. It
> describes what the system computes, how it gates and scores theories, the data it uses (with
> previews — the raw datasets are not shipped), the honest limitations, and the open problems where
> expert judgement is most valuable. Every claim maps to a named module in `crates/openqg-core/`.
> Companion docs in the same payload: `docs/ZYAL.md` (the orchestration layer), `docs/theory-league.md`
> (fair model selection), `docs/zyal-engine-rebuild.md` (the engine rebuild), `docs/zyal-next-level-design.md`
> (a stern internal critical review + roadmap).

---

## 1. What this is, and what we are trying to do

OpenQG is an **evidence-gated, "whitebox", derived-not-fit theory-discovery engine** for cosmology
and gravity. The ambition is to build the most trustworthy possible automated system for chasing the
physics frontier where quantum gravity / unification meets *data* — and to do it in a way a stern
referee would accept: every candidate theory is a structured physical object, every observable is a
genuine derivation (never an identity map), every survival decision is made by a deterministic oracle
(never by a language model), and every run is deterministic and replayable.

The design philosophy, in one line: **chase theories that are derivable, cross-domain consistent, and
robust — not theories that merely fit a curve.** Concretely, the engine refuses to reward a better
fit bought with an un-provenanced free parameter, and it scores model *classes* fairly (re-fitting the
baseline, penalizing complexity) rather than quoting a raw Δlog-likelihood against a fixed reference.

We are explicitly **not** claiming new physics. We are claiming an *engine* with honest gates and
honest scoring, and we want expert help making it more rigorous and pointing it at the
most-worth-chasing unified-physics targets.

## 3. The symbolic `Theory` (`theory/mod.rs`)

A candidate is **not** a vector of fitted numbers. It is a structured object:

- **`parameters: Vec<Parameter>`**, each with a typed **`Provenance`**: `Fundamental` |
  `Derived{mechanism}` | `Free`. `is_whitebox()` is true only for `Fundamental` or `Derived` with a
  non-empty mechanism. A `Free` parameter is a hard kill. This is the machine-checkable replacement
  for the old "does the prose avoid the word *fudge*" scan.
- **`alpha: AlphaBasis {α_M, α_B, α_K, α_T}`** — the linear effective-field-theory deviations from GR
  (Bellini & Sawicki 2014) on a Horndeski scalar-tensor background: Planck-mass run α_M, braiding
  α_B, kineticity α_K, tensor-speed excess α_T. GR ⇒ all zero.
- **`stability: Stability {kinetic_coefficient, q_s, sound_speed_sq, has_nondegenerate_higher_derivatives}`**
  — the no-ghost / no-gradient-instability / Ostrogradsky coefficients.
- **`screening: Option<String>`** — a declared mechanism (Vainshtein, chameleon, …) that recovers GR
  in the solar system.
- **`background: CosmologyParams {h, Ω_m, ω_b h², N_eff, Σm_ν, w0, wa, Ω_k, σ8, μ0}`** — the physical
  genes that drive the forward model.
- **`terms: Vec<Term>`** — action building blocks carrying `mass_dimension` and `free_lorentz_indices`
  for the cheap structural vetoes.

`Theory::baseline_lcdm()` is the GR+ΛCDM anchor (h=0.674, Ω_m=0.315, w=−1) that passes every gate.

## 4. The deterministic veto cascade (`theory/vetoes.rs`)

Cheapest-first structural gates; **any** violation ⇒ `final_fitness = 0` (the candidate is never
scored on data):

1. **Dimensional homogeneity** — every action term is mass-dimension 4.
2. **Lorentz invariance** — no uncontracted indices.
3. **Whitebox provenance** — no `Free` or empty-mechanism `Derived` parameter.
4. **GW170817 tensor speed** — `|α_T| ≤ 10⁻²` (Ezquiaga & Zumalacárregui 2017).
5. **Ghost / Ostrogradsky** — kinetic coefficient > 0, Q_s > 0, no non-degenerate higher derivatives.
6. **Gradient stability** — c_s² ≥ 0.
7. **PPN screening** — a gravity-modifying theory must declare a screening mechanism.

This is where "derived, not fit" is enforced *structurally*: `inject_free_parameter` (the gray-box
decoy) trips gate 3 and dies; a quintic-Galileon decoy trips gates 4–5.

## 5. The real forward model

The single seam is the `ForwardModel` trait; the default backend is `BackgroundForwardModel`
(pure-Rust, deterministic, no external solver). It returns a `PredictionRecord` **only** for
observables it can genuinely derive — an observable it cannot compute is *omitted*, never faked, so
"coverage" is honest.

### 5.1 Background (`cosmology/background.rs`)

Integrates the FLRW background from first principles:

- Expansion rate `E(z) = H(z)/H0 = sqrt(Ω_m(1+z)³ + Ω_r(1+z)⁴ + Ω_k(1+z)² + Ω_DE ρ_DE(z)/ρ_DE0)`
  with CPL dark energy `w(a) = w0 + wa(1−a)` and `ρ_DE(a)/ρ_DE0 = a^{−3(1+w0+wa)} e^{−3wa(1−a)}`;
  Ω_DE fixed by closure `Ω_m+Ω_r+Ω_k+Ω_DE = 1`.
- Distances by composite-Simpson integration of `1/E(z')` (2048 panels, sub-0.01% to z~3):
  comoving, transverse-comoving (curvature-aware), luminosity, distance modulus.
- Sound horizon `r_s = ∫ c_s/H dz` with `c_s = c/√(3(1+R))` (Eisenstein–Hu 1998), and `r_drag` via the
  Aubourg-2015 CAMB-calibrated fit (≈147.1 Mpc for Planck-2018), cross-checked against the EH98
  closed form to 3% in tests.
- Observables: BAO ratios `D_M/r_d, D_H/r_d, D_V/r_d`; SNe `μ(z)`; CMB *distance priors* `R` (shift)
  and `ℓ_A` (acoustic scale); BBN helium `Y_p` (linearized PRIMAT/PArthENoPE-consistent fit).

### 5.2 Linear growth of structure (`cosmology/growth.rs`)

This is the sector that actually constrains dark-energy dynamics and modified gravity. It integrates
the linear growth equation for the matter density contrast δ(a) by RK4 (512 steps, N = ln a):

```
   δ'' + (2 + dlnH/dN) δ' − (3/2) Ω_m(a) μ(a) δ = 0,    Ω_m(a) = Ω_m a⁻³ / E(a)²
```

Dark energy enters **exactly** through E(a); modified gravity enters through the effective
gravitational coupling for matter perturbations, taken in the GW170817-safe late-time
parametrization `μ(a) = G_eff/G = 1 + μ0 · ρ_DE(a)/ρ_DE0 / E(a)²` (normalized so μ0 is the deviation
today; Planck-2018 MG, arXiv:1807.06209). GR ⇒ μ≡1. Observables: the growth rate `f = dlnδ/dN`, the
RSD combination `fσ8(z) = σ8 · [δ(a)/δ(1)] · f(a)`, and the weak-lensing amplitude
`S8 = σ8 √(Ω_m/0.3)`. Validated against `f(z=0) ≈ Ω_m^0.55` and the observed fσ8 band.

The full CMB `C_ℓ` (TT/TE/EE), the lensing potential, and nonlinear `P(k)` are **deliberately
omitted** — they require an optional Boltzmann backend (CLASS / hi_class), which is the single
biggest fidelity gap (§9).

## 6. Scoring: fair model selection (`scoring/`, `theory/league.rs`)

### 6.1 Covariance-aware likelihood (`scoring/covariance.rs`)

The likelihood is a block-covariance multivariate Gaussian, `ln L = −½ rᵀ C⁻¹ r` per correlated
block (Cholesky solve, marginalizing over any observable the model cannot predict), with an
independent-diagonal term for everything not in a block. With no blocks it reduces exactly to the
diagonal likelihood. (Real covariance matrices are kept out of git per data policy; the committed
fixtures are diagonal, and the off-diagonal DESI/Planck correlations are a labelled follow-up via a
`--covariance` file — see §9.)

### 6.2 The league: profile-fit + information criteria (`theory/league.rs`, `theory league` CLI)

The evolution loop is a *search heuristic*; the **league** is the rigorous adjudicator. Each model is
a `ModelClass` (fixed structural form + a list of free parameters). Every model — **including the
baseline ΛCDM** — is profile-fit to the *same* data to its maximum likelihood by deterministic
Nelder–Mead, then ranked by AIC (`2k−2lnL`), BIC (`k ln n − 2lnL`), and the Schwarz/Laplace
log-evidence `ln Z ≈ −½ BIC`. ΔAIC / Δln Z vs ΛCDM is the headline — **not** a raw Δlog-likelihood
against a fixed reference.

**Why this matters (a correction we made to our own headline).** An earlier flagship run reported a
champion that "beats ΛCDM by +36.7 log-likelihood units." That number was an artifact: a *fitted*
candidate scored against a *fixed* Planck baseline under a *diagonal* likelihood with no complexity
penalty. Re-fitting ΛCDM fairly on the same 15 geometry observables inverts the story:

| model | k | χ² | ΔAIC vs ΛCDM | Δln Z |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **ΛCDM** (re-fit) | 2 | 16.42 | 0 (best) | 0 |
| w0waCDM | 4 | 12.65 | **+0.24** | **−0.83** |
| wCDM | 3 | 15.65 | +1.24 | −0.97 |

The raw χ² improvement of CPL over a *re-fit* ΛCDM is ~3.8 (not ~73), and once its two extra
parameters are penalized, evolving dark energy is **disfavored** on geometry alone. The engine now
responds correctly to *which* data it is given:

- **Geometry only** (DESI DR1 BAO + Planck priors + BBN, n=15): ΛCDM wins.
- **+ growth** (RSD fσ8 + DES-Y3 S8, n=21): at most a marginal evolving-DE hint (ΔAIC ≈ −1.2,
  Δln Z ≈ −0.44); modified-growth μ0 is **degenerate with σ8** here and not independently favored.
- **+ local H0** (SH0ES ladder, n=22): ΛCDM is badly stressed (χ² 27→44 — the Hubble tension,
  quantified) and a dark-energy extension is strongly favored (ΔAIC ≈ −16, Δln Z ≈ +7).

This is the kind of defensible, data-dependent statement the project is built to produce.

### 6.3 ε/β dual fitness, unification, evolution (`theory/{evaluate,unification,evolve,adversary}.rs`)

For the *search* (not the final adjudication), each survivor gets a dual objective: **ε** =
Δlog-likelihood vs baseline, squashed and **gated by β** = a derivability score
(`0.4·provenance + 0.4·stability-margin + 0.2·α_T-margin`), so a better fit only counts if the theory
is derivable. A **cross-domain unification** channel (`MIN` over GW speed, solar-system PPN, lab
fifth-force/EP, BBN, GW-friction siren) gates the result by its *weakest* sector. A **MAP-Elites**
archive keeps the best candidate per behavioural cell (quality-diversity). A co-evolving **adversary**
raises a frontier each generation that the champion must keep clearing; it now acts as a real
*survival threshold* on champion eligibility (a prior version was telemetry-only — see
`docs/zyal-next-level-design.md` §1.1). The unification channel is currently *analytic self-consistency*,
not independent data — another labelled gap (§9).

## 7. Orchestration: ZYAL, and "LLM proposes, host judges"

The autonomous loop is governed by **ZYAL** (see `docs/ZYAL.md` for the full account; the term is
overloaded across two repos). The load-bearing invariant: a language model may **propose** a theory
and **critique** it, but it **never** judges — every LLM output clears a deterministic oracle (the
veto cascade, the numeric forward model, a quality gate) before it can change a result. Proposals
enter as a strict-JSON AST through a derivation-checker that demotes any unverifiable "derived"
parameter to `Free` (which the whitebox veto then kills). Runs are deterministic in a seed and fully
logged.

## 9. Honest limitations and open problems (where expert judgement is most valuable)

We are deliberately explicit about what is *not* yet trustworthy, because that is exactly where we
want a physicist's steer:

1. **Background+growth only; no Boltzmann CMB.** No full `C_ℓ` (TT/TE/EE), no lensing, no nonlinear
   `P(k)`. The modified-gravity α_K/α_B sectors are therefore under-constrained, and the
   modified-growth μ0 is degenerate with σ8 on current fσ8/S8 data. Wiring a Boltzmann backend
   (CLASS/hi_class) is the single biggest fidelity step.
2. **Diagonal likelihoods in committed runs.** The covariance machinery exists and is tested, but the
   shipped fixtures are diagonal; the real DESI BAO intra-tracer and Planck `R/ℓ_A/ω_b` covariances
   are not yet populated. Quoted Δχ² should be read with that caveat.
3. **Analytic "unification", not independent data.** The cross-domain channel is closed-form
   self-consistency (α_T, screening flag, Y_p, a siren ratio), not a likelihood against real LVK
   sirens / MICROSCOPE / Eöt-Wash / GW170817 Δt.
4. **Leading theories not yet all scoreable.** ΛCDM, wCDM, w0waCDM, and a leading-order screened-MG
   class are in the league; **Early Dark Energy** and **coupled/interacting dark energy** are
   deliberately *not* faked as fitted knobs — they await derived scalar-field / coupling sectors.
5. **"Derived, not fit" binds form, not value.** The provenance gate forbids *adding* free knobs, but
   the continuous parameters of an allowed model class (h, Ω_m, w0, …) are still fit to data. A true
   value-level derivation oracle (e.g. an AI-Hilbert / Positivstellensatz certificate) is future work.
6. **Two engines; legacy retirement pending.** The legacy float-vector genome with an identity
   forward map still exists in `openqg-bench`; the new engine is additive and the cutover is planned.
7. **The science question itself.** Which unified-physics direction is *most worth chasing* on data we
   can realistically obtain, and how to score it honestly, is exactly the judgement we are seeking.
