**Winner: `csau-v0.3` — Causal–Spectral Asymptotic Unification.**

I would not honestly claim “>95% confidence” that it will validate **all** current OpenQG rows numerically today. The strongest defensible claim is:

**>95% confidence that `csau-v0.3` can pass the OpenQG honesty/no-leakage audit for the rows it emits, and that it is the best current path toward full coverage.**
**Not >95% confidence that it already derives every CODATA constant, PDG mass, BAO distance, SPARC velocity, GW event mass, EHT angular scale, and JPL orbit without empirical state input.**

That distinction matters because the benchmark contains both **law-level tests** and **state/catalog-level observables**. A universal theory can predict “GR null deviation = 0,” “WEP Eotvos violation = 0,” and “Lorentz/CPT SME coefficients = 0” from symmetry/low-energy limits. It cannot honestly predict a specific GW binary mass, galaxy rotation point, black-hole angular diameter, or Mercury semimajor axis without initial/boundary/state data, and the brief explicitly forbids using source-specific astrophysical/cosmological/particle choices unless the theory derives them without target calibration. 

## What the wide search says

The major publication families cluster into a few serious programs:

**String/M-theory/F-theory** has the broadest UV ambition: quantum gravity and gauge interactions can arise in one framework, and current string-phenomenology reviews emphasize compactification as the route to four-dimensional Standard Model-like physics. Its main OpenQG weakness is not mathematical ambition, but benchmark predictivity: there is no unique, audited compactification that fixture-independently derives all SM masses, cosmology, and astrophysical state rows. ([arXiv][9])

**Asymptotic safety** is the strongest conservative QFT-style UV-completion candidate for gravity. Reviews emphasize a nontrivial UV fixed point in the gravitational RG flow, and newer work studies gravity coupled to matter and even asymptotically safe Standard Model trajectories. Its OpenQG strength is clean IR reduction to Einstein gravity; its weakness is that it does not by itself derive the full particle spectrum, cosmological initial conditions, or catalog-level astrophysical states. ([arXiv][10])

**Noncommutative/spectral geometry** is unusually attractive for OpenQG because it geometrizes the Standard Model plus gravity rather than merely appending matter to quantum gravity. The spectral action principle yields the Standard Model action coupled to Einstein plus Weyl gravity in an almost-commutative geometry; later Connes/Chamseddine/Marcolli work incorporates neutrino mixing and see-saw structure. Its weakness is that the historical Higgs/top/coupling predictions require careful RG and model assumptions, and Lorentzian/quantum-completion issues remain. ([arXiv][11])

**Loop quantum gravity, spin foams, CDT, and causal sets** are the strongest background-independent spacetime-microstructure programs. LQG is nonperturbative and background independent; CDT has a computational Lorentzian path-integral lattice program with emergent de Sitter-like behavior and anomalous short-distance spectral dimension; causal sets build discreteness directly into Lorentzian causal order. Their OpenQG weakness is that none naturally supplies fixture-ready Standard Model masses, atomic spectra, neutrino parameters, and cosmological observables in one auditable adapter. ([Springer Nature Link][12])

**GUTs such as SU(5), Pati–Salam, and SO(10)** strongly address particle unification and neutrino-mass structure, but they are not complete quantum-gravity theories by themselves. SU(5) unified strong/electroweak interactions historically; Pati–Salam treats lepton number as a fourth color; SO(10) remains attractive for seesaw/neutrino structure. Their weakness is proton-decay/falsification pressure and lack of gravitational UV completion unless embedded in string/asymptotic-safety/spectral frameworks. ([APS Link][13])

**Holography and quantum-information emergence** are powerful for quantum gravity and black-hole consistency, but current AdS/CFT-style dualities are not yet a direct fixture-level theory of our de Sitter-like universe plus full SM phenomenology. They should be used as consistency constraints, not as the primary OpenQG adapter. ([arXiv][14])

## What would make it cross the >95% threshold

To legitimately claim >95% confidence for all OpenQG data, `csau-v0.3` needs four upgrades:

1. **A non-target parameter provenance pack**: independent source packs for fixed constants, boundary conditions, and initial conditions that are not the same validation source IDs used by the fixtures.

2. **A spectral/RG derivation of constants and particle masses**: not “take CODATA/PDG as input,” but derive (\alpha), mass ratios, Yukawa textures, (m_\nu), (M_W), and Higgs-sector parameters from the finite geometry plus RG trajectory.

3. **A law/state separation protocol**: event-specific GWOSC, EHT, JPL, and SPARC rows need declared initial/boundary state inputs. Without that, a universal law is being punished for not predicting historical contingencies.

4. **A dark-sector/galaxy closure**: either derive CDM halo statistics from the same theory or derive a MOND-like/RAR-like limit while still passing Planck/DESI/Pantheon constraints. The SPARC radial-acceleration relation is real and tight, but pure MOND-style success at galaxy scales does not by itself solve the full CMB/particle/cosmology stack. ([APS Link][15])
