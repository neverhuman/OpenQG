# 2. Human theory artifact: `NCG-SSM-OQG-v1`

## Core primitive

The primitive is a **real even spectral triple**:

[
(\mathcal A,\mathcal H,D,J,\gamma),
]

with product geometry

[
M\times F,
]

where (M) is an ordinary four-dimensional spin manifold and (F) is a finite noncommutative geometry. In the Standard Model version,

[
\mathcal A_F=\mathbb C\oplus\mathbb H\oplus M_3(\mathbb C).
]

The bosonic action is the spectral action and the fermionic action is the Dirac action:

[
S_{\rm bos}=\operatorname{Tr}, f(D_A/\Lambda),
]

[
S_{\rm ferm}=\frac12\langle J\Psi,D_A\Psi\rangle,
]

with fluctuated Dirac operator

[
D_A=D+A+JAJ^{-1},
\qquad
A=\sum_i a_i[D,b_i].
]

The original spectral-action paper states the universal action formula and says that applying it to the noncommutative space of the Standard Model gives the Standard Model action coupled to Einstein plus Weyl gravity, with high-scale relations among gauge couplings and the Higgs self-coupling. ([arXiv][4])

## Published method basis

The 2006 Chamseddine–Connes–Marcolli model presents an effective unified theory based on noncommutative geometry for the Standard Model with neutrino mixing, minimally coupled to gravity; it says the product of ordinary spacetime with a finite noncommutative geometry accounts for the Standard Model structure and spontaneous symmetry breaking. ([arXiv][5]) Connes’s 2006 paper says the KO-dimension-6 finite geometry addresses the finite-geometry input problem, fermion doubling, and generates the full Standard Model coupled with gravity, neutrino mixing, and a see-saw mechanism. ([arXiv][6]) A modern open-access treatment states that the full Standard Model Lagrangian can be derived from the almost-commutative manifold (M\times F_{\rm SM}). ([Springer][7])
