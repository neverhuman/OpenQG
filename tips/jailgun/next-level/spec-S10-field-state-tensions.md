## Repo-grounded starting point

I read the requested files as an external-field calibration exercise, not as an implementation audit. The engine’s current evidence base is narrow but explicit: DESI DR1 BAO distance ratios with covariance blocks, Planck 2018 distance-prior observables, five RSD fσ8 points, one SH0ES H0 datum, one DES Y3 S8 datum, and BBN/Ωb priors (`data/fixtures/cosmology/README.md`; `bao-desi-dr1.jsonl`; `cmb-planck18.jsonl`; `growth-rsd.jsonl`; `sh0es-h0.jsonl`; `wl-s8.jsonl`; `covariance/desi-dr1-bao.json`; `covariance/planck18-distance-priors.json`). That is a defensible V7 evidence skeleton, not a field-complete cosmology likelihood.

The paper is much more honest than the older result artifacts. It records the destructive audit trail: V4 killed for rediscovery/tie-credit exploits; V5 killed for free background drift and bare screening; V6 killed because a headline novelty witness was riding the oracle’s own biased CMB acoustic-scale fitting formula; and V7 stands at about 43/100 as a suppressed-growth/dark-scattering phenomenology that bends S8/fσ8 in the right direction but does not clear the evidence bar (`paper/main.tex`, Abstract/Introduction/Results). `docs/V6-CAMPAIGN-REPORT.md` gives the crucial number: the apparent H0/evolving-DE win collapsed when the engine found its own `l_A` offset of `+0.755`, about `8.4σ`. `docs/zyal-next-level-design.md` is also blunt: the old “co-evolving adversary” was not outcome-changing; the “symbolic discovery” loop was mostly float evolution over fixed forms; and the old `+36.7` result was not a fair model comparison. That honesty is the asset. The risk is that the paper still wants to stand beside mature human cosmology pipelines while using compressed, incomplete likelihoods.

## 1. H0, with numbers: where the field stands

**CMB/BAO/BBN inverse ladder.** Planck 2018 ΛCDM remains the canonical low-H0 anchor, `H0≈67.4±0.5 km s^-1 Mpc^-1` [Planck 2018 parameters, arXiv:1807.06209]. ACT DR6 plus Planck/lensing/DESI/Pantheon+ analyses continue to find broad consistency with standard recombination, GR growth, and a cosmological constant, with no collaboration-level need for H0-raising extensions [ACT DR6 extended models, arXiv:2503.14454; confidence high for “no ACT-only discovery,” verify final journal tables]. DESI BAO plus BBN gives an inverse-distance-ladder value around `H0≈68.5±0.8`, independent of Planck acoustic-peak physics at leading order [DESI DR1/DR2 BAO cosmology papers, arXiv:2404.03002, arXiv:2503.14738; DESI 2025 guide; confidence high]. This is exactly why the repo’s single SH0ES scalar is not enough: a late-time candidate that lifts H0 to 73 must also pass the BAO+BBN inverse ladder, not just the Planck distance-prior object in `cmb-planck18.jsonl`.

**SH0ES Cepheids.** The Cepheid/SN Ia ladder remains high. The archive uses Riess et al. 2022, `H0=73.04±1.04` (`data/fixtures/cosmology/sh0es-h0.jsonl`; Riess et al., arXiv:2112.04510). The post-JWST SH0ES line claims no Cepheid crowding bias large enough to remove the tension. A 2025 “Perfect Host” style result reports JWST+HST Cepheid distances and `H0≈73.5±0.9`, with variants using TRGB calibrations still near `73.2±0.9` [SH0ES/JWST Cepheids, arXiv:2509.01667; confidence medium-high because the direction is clear but the exact paper/version should be live-checked before publication]. If the project claims to resolve H0, it must specify whether it is resolving **SH0ES Cepheid H0** or the multi-calibrator local-distance network.

**CCHP/JWST TRGB and JAGB.** The Chicago-Carnegie Hubble Program has moved in the opposite direction from SH0ES. Freedman et al. 2024 report a current-best TRGB value `H0=70.39±1.22(stat)±1.33(sys)±0.70(SN)`; using JWST-only TRGB gives `68.81±1.79(stat)±1.32(sys)`, and JWST-only JAGB gives `67.80±2.17(stat)±1.64(sys)` [CCHP/JWST TRGB+JAGB, arXiv:2408.06153; confidence high for the quoted pre-2025 values]. The field implication is severe: “local ladder” is not a monolith. A theory that simply drives H0 to SH0ES may worsen the CCHP/JAGB agreement with inverse ladders. OpenQG should not assign one H0 observable the authority to define the sector.

**TDCOSMO time delays.** Strong-lens time-delay cosmography does not currently arbitrate the tension by itself. Under H0LiCOW/TDCOSMO power-law or composite mass profiles, seven lenses produced high values near `H0≈73–74` with ≈2–3% precision [Millon et al., arXiv:1912.08027]. When Birrer et al. relaxed the mass-profile assumption and used hierarchical TDCOSMO+SLACS information, the result moved to `H0=67.4^{+4.1}_{-3.2}`; TDCOSMO-only flexible modeling was `74.5^{+5.6}_{-6.1}` [TDCOSMO IV, arXiv:2007.02941]. A 2026 “soup-to-nuts” strong-lensing measurement advertises ≈6.5% precision for an end-to-end lens [arXiv:2604.14145; confidence low-medium, very near cutoff, verify final sample/status]. The engine should score time delays as a **profile-prior-dependent likelihood family**, not as a single H0 number.

**Megamasers and sirens.** The Megamaser Cosmology Project gives an independent geometric H0 close to the high ladder but with large uncertainty: commonly `H0≈73.9±3.0`, with values ranging roughly 72–77 depending on peculiar-velocity treatment [Pesce et al. 2020, arXiv:2001.09213]. Gravitational-wave sirens remain too broad to settle the issue: GW170817 gave roughly `H0=70^{+12}_{-8}` [Abbott et al. 2017, Nature]; GWTC-3 dark-siren/population analyses remained at order 10–20% precision [LVK GWTC-3 cosmology, Phys. Rev. X 13, 041039 context; confidence medium]; a 2026 GWTC-5 cosmology preprint reports a 25.7% reduction in H0 uncertainty versus GWTC-4 but still no competitive sub-percent decision [LVK, arXiv:2605.27227; confidence medium-low because this is cutoff-adjacent and must be live-checked].

**Viable resolution classes.** The Schöneberg et al. “H0 Olympics” framework is still the right diagnostic: early-time solutions must alter pre-recombination sound-horizon physics; late-time solutions must alter distances/growth after recombination without breaking BAO/SNe/growth; many models fail once all data are combined [Schöneberg et al., arXiv:2107.10291]. The later “multiple H0s”/CosmoVerse synthesis sharpened the sociological point: there are two robust camps around ≈68 and ≈73, with systematics still possible, but if both camps are right then simple one-knob late-time fixes are disfavored [CosmoVerse white paper, arXiv:2504.01669; confidence medium, broad synthesis]. After ACT DR6, vanilla EDE is not the consensus winner. ACT DR6 extended-model analyses find no collaboration-level evidence for EDE or related H0-raising sectors [arXiv:2503.14454]. Pro-EDE reanalyses argue that ACT DR6+DESI DR2 can still allow or even prefer nonzero EDE under specific low-ℓ/profile-likelihood choices, with values like `f_EDE≈0.09±0.03` and `H0≈71.0±1.1` without SH0ES in one analysis [Poulin et al., arXiv:2505.08051; confidence medium; verify likelihood choices and ACT collaboration response]. For OpenQG, the claim should be: **the engine can audit resolution classes; it has not discovered an H0 resolution.**

## 2. S8 and growth: status of the tension and V7 plausibility

The S8/growth picture is more alive than “solved,” but less clean than a discovery. Planck ΛCDM predicts high clustering, historically `S8≈0.83`. DES Y3 measured `S8=0.776±0.017`, which is the value in the repo (`data/fixtures/cosmology/wl-s8.jsonl`; DES Y3 3x2pt, arXiv:2105.13549). DES Y6 is now the more important comparison: its 3x2pt analysis reports `S8=0.789±0.012` and a projected S8 difference from CMB at about `2.6σ`, while full-parameter-space consistency is milder, around `1.8σ` [DES Y6 3x2pt, arXiv:2601.14559; confidence medium, very fresh, verify final collaboration release]. KiDS-Legacy points in the other direction: the final KiDS analysis reports consistency with CMB in ΛCDM and a combined all-external-datasets value around `S8=0.816±0.006`; it does not support a strong w0wa rescue for shear+CMB [KiDS-Legacy, arXiv:2512.11041; confidence medium, cutoff-adjacent]. A 2026 synthesis quotes CMB `S8≈0.836^{+0.012}_{-0.013}`, DES Y6 tension around 2.4–2.7σ, and KiDS-Legacy below 1σ tension, which is a fair verbal summary: survey systematics and analysis choices matter, but new physics is not excluded [S8 review, arXiv:2602.12238; confidence medium].

RSD and full-shape data matter more for V7 than a single S8 number. The repo uses five fσ8 measurements from 6dFGS/BOSS/eBOSS (`growth-rsd.jsonl`). DESI DR1 full-shape analyses are much stronger. One DESI full-shape result reports, from DESI FS+BAO+BBN, `Ωm=0.2962±0.0095` and `σ8=0.842±0.034`; adding CMB gives `Ωm=0.3056±0.0049` and `σ8=0.8121±0.0053` [DESI DR1 full-shape, arXiv:2411.12022; confidence high-medium]. In modified-gravity parameterizations, DESI+CMB+DESY3 gives `μ0≈0.04±0.22` and `Σ0≈0.044±0.047` in one paper [same]. Another DESI MG analysis gives `μ0=0.05±0.22`, `Σ0=0.008±0.045` in ΛCDM, and in w0wa `μ0=-0.24^{+0.32}_{-0.28}`, `Σ0=0.006±0.043`, all consistent with GR [DESI DR1 MG, arXiv:2411.12026; confidence high-medium]. That does not kill suppressed growth, but it prices it: the V7 class cannot claim external confirmation unless it predicts a pattern inside these broad but nonzero constraints.

The V7 champion class should receive an **external plausibility score of 35/100 today**: plausible enough to keep, far below publishable discovery. Positives: it targets the only tension sector where the engine’s low-score survivor is directionally aligned with published anomalies; Nguyen, Huterer, and Wen style growth-index fits have argued for suppressed growth relative to GR, with γ higher than the GR value ≈0.55 [Nguyen, Huterer & Wen, PRL 2023, arXiv:2302.01331]; dark-scattering/dark-drag models are a real lineage, beginning with Simpson 2010 and expanded through Pourtsidou/Skordis/Copeland and later dark-sector momentum-exchange work. Recent dark-scattering analyses emphasize that pure momentum exchange between dark matter and dark energy can affect growth while leaving the background and CMB less disturbed, and that DES Y3+BOSS/BAO combinations now constrain this sector [dark scattering constraints, arXiv:2502.03390; Poulin et al. “The sigma-8 tension is a drag,” Phys. Rev. D 107, 123538]. Negatives: the current ledger is not a dark-scattering theory. The smoke proposal is a `planck_mu0` phenomenological suppressed-growth object with `mu0=-0.12` and expected `G_eff/G≈0.88` (`ops/jailgun/next-level-payload/results/v7-smoke-proposal-ledger.jsonl`). It does not derive a momentum-transfer term, perturbation equations, lensing response, nonlinear matter spectrum, or CMB-lensing signature. External constraints are already strong enough that a generic suppression knob can be hidden in error bars but not celebrated.

Engineering implication: keep the V7 lineage as a **candidate mechanism class**, but split it into three certificates: (1) phenomenological μ/Σ or growth-index suppression, (2) a true dark-scattering/dark-drag model with Euler-equation drag and background conservation, and (3) a data-likelihood survival certificate against DESI full-shape, DES Y6, KiDS-Legacy, Planck/ACT/SPT lensing. Until all three exist, the paper may say: “V7 rediscovered a plausible direction under strict economics.” It may not say: “V7 found a viable dark-scattering solution.”

## 3. Evolving dark energy: DESI, SNe, and what settles it

DESI changed the tone but not yet the standard model. DESI DR1 BAO created the first high-profile w0wa excitement: DESI+CMB alone already preferred dynamical dark energy at roughly the 2–3σ level, and adding SNe could raise the apparent preference depending on the SN compilation [DESI DR1, arXiv:2404.03002; DESI cosmology interpretation, arXiv:2404.03002 companion papers]. DESI DR2 strengthened and complicated the story. The DR2 BAO cosmology paper uses more than 14 million galaxies and quasars; BAO is well described by flat ΛCDM, but the BAO parameters are in mild `2.3σ` tension with CMB in ΛCDM, w0wa alleviates the tension, DESI+CMB gives about `3.1σ` preference for w0wa, and adding SNe gives a range from about `2.8σ` to `4.2σ` depending on the sample [DESI DR2 BAO, arXiv:2503.14738; Phys. Rev. D 112, 083515; confidence high for DESI-reported numbers]. The extended dark-energy analysis reports that evidence is strongest at `z≲0.3`, often favoring a phantom-crossing-like history, but not uniquely [DESI DR2 extended dark energy, arXiv:2503.14743; confidence high-medium].

The SN dependence is the core. Pantheon+, Union3, and DES-SN5YR do not pull identically. DESI’s dark-energy significance is largest for some combinations and weaker for others; profile-likelihood and SN-systematics critiques argue that Gaussian compressed constraints and prior-volume choices can overstate evidence for w0wa. The repo currently has no SN covariance likelihood (`paper/main.tex`, Limitations), so OpenQG cannot honestly calibrate an evolving-DE claim. `docs/theory-league.md` already reports the right internal warning: geometry-only DESI DR1 BAO+Planck priors+BBN still lets ΛCDM win; adding growth still lets ΛCDM win; only adding local SH0ES H0 makes w0waCDM win with `ΔAIC≈-14` and `ΔlnZ≈+5.9`. That is a statement about the H0-ladder pull, not a robust autonomous discovery of evolving dark energy.

DES Y6 adds an independent, very fresh low-redshift view. The DES Y6 multi-probe paper reports `w0=-0.84±0.10` and `wa=-0.44^{+0.60}_{-0.55}`, described as about a `2.2σ` deviation from a cosmological constant from DES alone [DES Y6 multi-probe, arXiv:2605.27221; confidence medium-low because it is extremely near cutoff and should be verified against the final collaboration paper]. That is not enough to declare ΛCDM dead. It is enough to make SN+BAO covariance fidelity a top V8 requirement.

What settles it? DESI DR3/extended years, Euclid DR1/DR2, and Rubin/LSST SNe + weak lensing will determine whether the low-z w(z) pull is a coherent expansion-history feature or a cross-calibration/systematics artifact. For OpenQG, pre-positioning means implementing SNe and BAO likelihoods now, freezing predictions before releases, and not tuning to the collaboration’s post-release contours.

## 4. MG searches — competition and honest comparison

The viable modified-gravity space after GW170817 is narrower but not empty. Luminal gravitational-wave speed killed broad Horndeski/Galileon sectors with anomalous tensor speed, but left scalar-tensor, beyond-Horndeski/DHOST subspaces, f(R)-like screening limits, nDGP-like braneworld phenomenology, coupled/dark-sector interactions, and EFT-of-DE parameterizations that preserve `c_T≈1` [GW170817 speed constraints: Abbott et al. 2017; Baker et al., Creminelli & Vernizzi, Ezquiaga & Zumalacárregui 2017]. Human teams usually scan this space with mature likelihoods: Cobaya/CAMB/CLASS/MontePython/CosmoMC for baseline cosmology; hi_class and EFTCAMB/EFTCosmoMC for Horndeski/EFT-DE; MGCAMB/ISiTGR-like μ–Σ phenomenology; EFTofLSS/full-shape pipelines for galaxy clustering; DES pipelines such as CosmoSIS/Firecrown; KiDS likelihood stacks; and DESI blinded BAO/full-shape/MG pipelines. Named groups include DESI, DES, KiDS, ACT, SPT, Planck, Euclid theory/lensing groups, TDCOSMO/H0LiCOW, SH0ES, CCHP, Megamaser Cosmology Project, and LVK cosmology.

Current constraints are not friendly to broad MG claims. DESI MG constraints quoted above are consistent with GR at the 1σ level for μ0/Σ0. Planck PR4 ISW-lensing work reports that adding ISW-lensing tightens MG parameter constraints by roughly 20%, shrinks viable parameter space by 50–80% in explored parameterizations, and remains consistent with GR at 95% when combined with ACT lensing, DESI DR1 BAO, and SNe [Planck PR4 ISW-lensing MG, arXiv:2503.09893; confidence medium]. ACT DR6 extended-model analyses similarly find no evidence for late-time growth changes [arXiv:2503.14454]. In an α-basis, DESI reports constraints such as `c_M<1.14` in a no-braiding case and a mild `c_B>0` feature requiring further study, not a discovery [DESI MG, arXiv:2411.12026].

What does OpenQG do that they do not? It can autonomously generate, adversarially audit, and permanently regression-test theory proposals under explicit honesty economics. The V6 failure is exactly the kind of thing human pipelines rarely advertise as a win: the system found that its own CMB formula bias had manufactured a false novelty witness (`paper/main.tex`; `docs/V6-CAMPAIGN-REPORT.md`). It also formalizes “degree of freedom pricing,” “tie with ΛCDM scores zero,” and “LLM proposes but deterministic host judges.” Those are genuine contributions if stated as workflow science, not cosmology evidence.

Where is it outclassed? Likelihood fidelity, perturbation theory, nonlinear modeling, survey systematics, covariance handling, and data coverage. DESI/DES/KiDS/Planck/ACT pipelines model selection functions, redshift distributions, intrinsic alignments, baryons, nonlinear clustering, photo-z, shear calibration, covariance validation, and blinding. OpenQG currently uses compressed observables and fitting formulas. The paper may claim a novel **search/audit architecture**; it may not claim parity with mature cosmological inference pipelines.

## 6. Twelve-to-twenty-four-month horizon and pre-positioning

**DESI DR3 / later DESI releases.** DESI completed its planned 3D mapping program in April 2026 with more than 47 million galaxies and quasars reported publicly; community schedules point to DR3 availability around late 2026 or early 2027, but the exact public-cosmology release date should be verified from DESI collaboration pages [DESI public updates 2026; confidence medium]. Pre-positioning: freeze BAO, RSD, full-shape, w0wa, μ–Σ, and dark-scattering observable definitions now. For any V7 descendant, publish signed predictions for DR3 fσ8 bins and μ/Σ constraints before the DR3 likelihood is public.

**Euclid.** ESA/Euclid schedules list Q2 data around June 24, 2026 and DR1 around October 21, 2026, with DR2 in 2029 and DR3 in 2031 [ESA/Euclid release schedule; confidence medium-high, verify dates near submission]. Euclid DR1 is not the final cosmology word, but it is the start of a high-precision external imaging/spectroscopy cross-check. Pre-positioning: implement Euclid-ready observable schemas for weak-lensing shear, galaxy clustering, cross-correlations, photo-z nuisance parameters, and covariance provenance.

**Rubin/LSST.** Rubin DP2 is expected in mid-2026, while LSST DR1 is based on the first year of survey data and appears roughly 12–14 months after survey start; exact first-cosmology timing depends on survey operations [Rubin data release documentation; confidence medium]. Pre-positioning: write sealed forecasts for SNe distance-modulus residual trends, shear S8, IA/baryon nuisance sensitivity, and cross-correlation tests. Do not tune to preview data and then call it prediction.

**ACT/SPT/Simons Observatory.** ACT DR6 final data and extended-model papers are already in the 2025 field state. SPT-3G and Simons Observatory will sharpen lensing, Neff, EDE, and growth tests across 2026–2028. Pre-positioning: for every early-time/H0 proposal, freeze predictions for damping-tail shifts, CMB lensing amplitude, Neff-like degeneracies, and sound-horizon changes. EDE without CMB-damping/lensing survival should be auto-vetoed.

**LVK sirens and LISA.** LVK O4/O5 sirens are the 12–24 month opportunity; LISA-era massive-black-hole sirens are not a near-term V8 validation event. Pre-positioning: define standard-siren likelihood adapters and keep them separate from EM ladders. LISA should be a schema/forecast target, not a near-term evidence claim.

The registry standard should be unforgiving: candidate ID, theory fingerprint, parameter vector, code hash, likelihood version, observable definition, timestamp, and human-readable null prediction. If a prediction is not sealed before the collaboration result is public, it is not a prediction.

## What we got wrong

1. **Oversold old evidence wins.** `prod-1000-champion.json` still contains an apparent `epsilon_delta_log_likelihood≈36.74` and high-looking champion metadata. `docs/zyal-next-level-design.md` and `docs/theory-league.md` correctly say this was not a fair model comparison. **Check that settles it:** run profile-fitted ΛCDM, w0waCDM, and the candidate against identical BAO+CMB+BBN+growth+H0+SNe likelihoods with equal nuisance treatment and report ΔAIC/ΔBIC/evidence; stale artifact numbers must be excluded from the paper abstract.

2. **Confusing phenomenological suppression with a dark-scattering mechanism.** The V7 ledger has a μ0-style suppressed-growth proposal, not a derived dark-sector momentum-transfer theory. **Check that settles it:** require equations of motion, perturbation equations, lensing response, matter-power prediction, and mapping to published dark-scattering parameters before using the phrase “dark scattering.”

3. **Treating H0 as one number.** The repo ingests SH0ES 2022 only. That makes the H0 sector artificially binary: Planck versus SH0ES. The outside field includes CCHP/JWST TRGB/JAGB, TDCOSMO, MCP, sirens, and inverse ladders with different systematics and central values. **Check that settles it:** the H0 panel backlog item must produce per-calibrator residuals and a combined covariance-aware score; a candidate that fits SH0ES while failing CCHP/inverse ladder cannot score as an H0 solution.

4. **Underpricing SNe in dark-energy claims.** DESI w0wa evidence is SN-compilation-dependent, and the paper itself admits SNe are absent. **Check that settles it:** implement Pantheon+, Union3, and DES-SN5YR full-covariance likelihoods as separate named score slices; require any evolving-DE claim to survive all three or explicitly state which one drives it.

5. **CMB compressed-likelihood overconfidence.** V6 proved that the internal CMB acoustic-scale fitting formula could create a false discovery. **Check that settles it:** no H0/EDE/MG novelty credit above a small cap until CLASS/CAMB residual envelopes are installed and regression-tested across the candidate’s parameter domain.

6. **Calling narrow coverage “field coverage.”** `data/fixtures/cosmology/README.md` is clear about the current fixtures, but prose around the project can sound broader than the evidence. **Check that settles it:** every paper claim must include the active data roster. If DES Y6, KiDS-Legacy, DESI full-shape, Pantheon+/Union3/DES-SN5YR, ACT/SPT lensing, or sirens were not ingested, the text must say so.

7. **Mistaking adversarial telemetry for adversarial science.** `docs/zyal-next-level-design.md` says the old co-evolving adversary did not change outputs. **Check that settles it:** a no-adversary ablation must produce measurably different killed/protected candidate distributions, or the adversary must be described as logging, not selection pressure.

8. **Possible near-cutoff drift in this S10 report.** DES Y6, KiDS-Legacy, DESI DR2 extended analyses, ACT DR6/EDE reanalyses, LVK GWTC-5, Euclid/Rubin dates, and 2026 strong-lensing work are close to the knowledge boundary. **Check that settles it:** before submission, live-verify the arXiv versions and collaboration pages named here, update central values/significances, and tag each with `confidence` in the paper’s external-claims ledger.
