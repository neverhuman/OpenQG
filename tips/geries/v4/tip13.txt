## 3. The no-leakage theorem

**Theorem: Strict Prediction Admissibility**

Let (r) be an OpenQG observable row. Let its theory extraction be

[
y_r=\mathcal E_r(\mathcal L,\theta,\sigma),
]

where (\theta) are theory parameters not derived by (\mathcal L+\mathcal C), and (\sigma) are source or boundary-state variables not derived by (\mathcal L+\mathcal C). If there exist two admissible pairs

[
(\theta_1,\sigma_1),\quad(\theta_2,\sigma_2)
]

such that

[
\mathcal E_r(\mathcal L,\theta_1,\sigma_1)
\ne
\mathcal E_r(\mathcal L,\theta_2,\sigma_2),
]

then any single numeric output for (r) requires extra information beyond the submitted theory. If that extra information is the fixture value, a validation row, benchmark feedback, a fitted coefficient, a reference predictor, or a copied empirical constant, the row is leakage. If the extra information is absent, the row must be `not_implemented`.

**Proof sketch:** If the extraction map is not constant over admissible unresolved parameters and states, the theory does not pick one value. Selecting one value is equivalent to selecting hidden (\theta), hidden (\sigma), or hidden data. That violates the no-calibration rule unless the selector is independently derived and documented.

This theorem turns the archive’s “law/state caution” into an executable criterion.

## 4. What previous agents assumed too quickly

The tips mostly made the same premature jumps.

They treated

[

as if choosing the finite algebra were the same as deriving it uniquely. It is not. Once chosen, it organizes (SU(3)), (SU(2)), (U(1)), chirality, color, and anomaly cancellation well. But the choice itself is still a structural input unless a uniqueness theorem proves it.

They treated asymptotic safety as if “UV fixed point exists” meant “low-energy constants are fixed.” That only follows if the relevant critical surface is zero-dimensional or if all relevant directions are fixed by independent constraints. Reviews of asymptotic safety present it as a serious UV-completion program with open questions, not as a completed derivation of every Standard Model and cosmological parameter. ([Frontiers][1])

They treated RG equations as constants engines. But

[

does not derive (\alpha), (g_1,g_2,g_3), or (\Lambda_{\rm QCD}) unless the boundary condition, matching scale, thresholds, matter content, and scheme are derived.

They treated the finite Dirac operator (D_F) as if putting Yukawas inside (D_F) derives Yukawas. It does not. A slot for (Y_u,Y_d,Y_e,Y_\nu) is not a spectrum.

They treated

[
V_{\rm CKM}=U_u^\dagger U_d

and

[
U_{\rm PMNS}=U_e^\dagger U_\nu

as if they derive mixing. They only define mixing after the mass matrices are known.

They treated Friedmann equations as cosmological predictions. But Planck, DESI, Pantheon+, and BAO rows require a derived cosmological state: (H_0,\Omega_i,A_s,n_s,r,\tau,r_d,\rho_\Lambda), and source/calibration models. Planck and DESI are empirical constraint surfaces, not derivation sources for a strict theory. ([arXiv][2])

They treated catalog rows as law rows. GWOSC event masses, EHT angular diameters, SPARC velocities, JPL orbital elements, NIST ASD levels, and Pantheon+ moduli are state-conditioned.
