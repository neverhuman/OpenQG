# OpenQG Moonshot

## North Star

OpenQG exists to make quantum-gravity ideas easier to test, compare, and
improve in the open. Its highest ambition is to turn a field often limited by
fragmented theory, fragile data links, and hard-to-repeat claims into a shared
evidence engine for serious scientific progress.

## What OpenQG Could Become

OpenQG could become the public benchmark commons for quantum gravity: a place
where theories, observables, datasets, predictions, and scorecards meet under
clear contracts. It could let researchers ask sharper questions, publish
falsifiable claims with durable provenance, and see how candidate ideas behave
against the same transparent evidence.

At its best, OpenQG is not a gatekeeper for one school of thought. It is
neutral infrastructure for disciplined comparison, built so bold theoretical
work can be exposed to measurement, uncertainty, revision, and reproducible
criticism.

## Why It Matters

Quantum gravity needs imagination, but imagination compounds only when claims
can survive contact with shared tests. OpenQG can help shorten the distance
between a proposed idea and the evidence needed to challenge it.

If the project succeeds, it gives independent researchers, domain experts, and
tool builders a common surface for collaboration. The result is a healthier
research loop: more visible assumptions, better benchmark hygiene, clearer
failures, and stronger reasons to trust the rare signals that remain.

## What Success Looks Like

Success looks like a small but credible release that people can inspect, rerun,
and argue with. Over time, it looks like new theory candidates arriving with
explicit predictions, benchmark results carrying enough context to be audited,
and release artifacts becoming reliable scientific receipts rather than
one-off demonstrations.

The long-term win is cultural as much as technical: OpenQG helps make
quantum-gravity research more cumulative, more adversarial in the productive
sense, and more welcoming to ideas that can earn confidence through evidence.
