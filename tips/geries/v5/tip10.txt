law_null

structural_integer

unsupported

It emits `predicted` only if the row has a complete proof certificate:

```text
law proof passes
constant proof passes
state proof passes or state-independent
estimator proof passes or estimator-independent
unit proof passes
target-taint graph is empty
```

Otherwise it emits `not_implemented` or `invalid_domain`.

No unsupported row receives a numeric placeholder. No strict predicted row has nonempty `calibration_data_used`.
