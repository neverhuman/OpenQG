```text
derived_law
derived_structural_integer
metrology_anchor
unresolved_constant
independent_state
estimator_definition
```

A row can be `predicted` only if:

```text
1. no path exists from fixture_target, benchmark_feedback, or reference_predictor;
2. all constants on the path are derived or metrology-only;
3. all state nodes are either absent or independently source-certified;
4. the estimator is explicitly reproduced;
5. units and uncertainty propagation are declared;
6. the row shape matches the prediction semantics.
```

Otherwise the adapter emits `not_implemented`.

This is not optional engineering; it is the mathematical content of no-leakage.
