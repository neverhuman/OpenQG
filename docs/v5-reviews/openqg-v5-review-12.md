# V6 roadmap and milestones

Milestone 1: unify the physics gate. Before adding new search operators, make one function define promotability. Implement `physics_kills` in `vetoes.rs`, wire it into `evaluate.rs`, `physics_score.rs`, proposer scoring, contender scoring, and trust gates. Validation: the V5 champion JSON replay should now fail if it has nonzero alpha plus `screening_recovery=null`; a quantified sufficiently screened test theory should pass. External credibility condition: no scorecard may say "disqualified=false" while `adjudicate(theory)` returns a reason.

Milestone 2: close free background drift. Synchronize `Theory.parameters` with `Theory.background`, count background degrees of freedom, and require provenance for non-baseline `h`, `omega_m`, `w0`, `sigma8`, and MG fields. Validation: replay V5 chunks and confirm parsimony drops or candidates are disqualified unless background moves are explicitly derived. External credibility condition: a physicist can inspect a champion and see exactly which cosmological parameters moved, why, and what complexity cost was paid.

Milestone 3: wire covariance into production likelihood. Promote `LikelihoodData` from a core utility to the only production scoring input. Add Planck compressed CMB 3x3 covariance, BAO covariance blocks, and at least one SNe compressed covariance block. Validation: diagonal and block modes reproduce known toy cases; V5 `Delta lnZ about 55` is recomputed under covariance and reported side-by-side. External credibility condition: no headline evidence number is quoted from independent Gaussians for correlated surveys.

Milestone 4: repair novelty scoring. Split witness detectability from honesty tolerance, remove half novelty for no/uncomputable witnesses, and require holdout or non-fit observables for full novelty. Validation: an alpha-jitter theory with no witness gets zero novelty; an honest `fsigma8@0.51` witness on a fit observable gets "fit explanation" but not novel prediction; a new held-out high-z growth prediction can earn novelty. External credibility condition: novelty means a falsifiable computed prediction, not a metadata flag.

Milestone 5: make re-clothing a search operator, not a score merger. Implement `ReclothingCandidate` in `theory_population.rs` by building a fresh `ProposalDoc` from donor claims and descendant theory, marking dirty symbols, and running `score_proposal`. Validation: grafting a `mu0=-0.1` claim graph onto a `mu0=0` descendant fails; grafting onto a matching descendant passes only after witness values are recomputed. External credibility condition: every point of derivation/unification credit is re-earned on the exact scored background.

Milestone 6: diversify the proposer curriculum. Modify `proposer_memory.rs` so only promotable candidates become positive memory. Add family quotas or prompt lanes for phenomenological mu0, dark scattering, interacting dark energy, neutrino suppression, early-time sound-horizon changes, and null diagnostics. Validation: a 50-proposal campaign should not produce 49 variants of one registry relation unless the run was explicitly configured that way. External credibility condition: the engine explores multiple mechanism classes, not one prompt-induced attractor.

Milestone 7: registry expansion with mechanisms. Add dark-sector momentum-exchange growth drag as the first suppressed-growth mechanism, with a new growth friction coefficient rather than squeezing it into `mu0`. Add approximate massive-neutrino suppression only with a low rigor weight until a Boltzmann backend exists. Keep `planck_mu0_geff` at rigor 0.3. Validation: each relation has tests for correct value, wrong value, GR limit, domain failure, and bound forward-model consequence. External credibility condition: a high-rigor suppressed-growth candidate cites equations that the engine actually integrates.

Milestone 8: reporting and receipts. Update white papers to print parameter/background consistency, covariance mode, data roles, raw evidence values, adjudication receipt, and novelty audit verdicts. Validation: the report for a replayed V5 champion should visibly show why it is no longer promotable or why it paid parameter cost. External credibility condition: a reviewer can reproduce the score decomposition without reading hidden logs.

Milestone 9: external physicist smoke test. Freeze a V6 release candidate and ask an outside cosmologist to supply three decoys and two viable-but-boring baselines. The decoys should include diagonal-covariance overfit, unquantified screening, and fake novelty tolerance. The engine should kill or demote all three. The boring baselines should survive without novelty inflation. Only then run a new campaign.

A V6 champion becomes serious when it satisfies all of these: covariance-aware positive evidence, no free background drift, mandatory adjudication pass, at least one computed holdout prediction, mechanism-backed derivations with GR limits, and a report that separates "fits current tensions" from "new physical theory." Until then, the V5 result is a valuable adversarial diagnostic, not a discovery.
