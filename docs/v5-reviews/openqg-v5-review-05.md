# The 55 ceiling and re-clothing

The team's diagnosis of the 55 ceiling is directionally right: pure evolved children have fit without derivation structure, while LLM proposals have derivation-looking structure without optimized fit. In `run-data/v5-chunk-1-white-paper.json`, the champion decomposes as `data_fit=19.9993`, `novel_prediction=10`, `robustness=13`, `parsimony=12`, `derivation_rigor=0`, `unification=0`. This is exactly a bare theory scoring through `theory_population.rs::score_theory`, which supplies an empty `ClaimGraph`, empty obligations, empty evidence store, and empty `UnificationClaim`.

But the proposed "re-clothe" step is dangerous if it grafts claim graphs after selection. The central invariant of V5 is supposed to be that claims bind into the background the model computes. A graft can violate that invariant by earning derivation/unification on one background and data fit on another. Example: a jekko proposal may certify `planck_mu0_geff` with `mu0=-0.1` and declare a suppressed-growth witness at Planck-ish `h=0.674`, while its evolved descendant reaches the data-fit maximum by moving `h=0.718`, `omega_m=0.282`, `w0=-1.14`, and alpha fields. Copying the proposal's obligations onto the descendant would collect structure credit on a different physical theory.

If re-clothing exists, graft time must be a full re-verification, not a metadata merge. Specifically, in `crates/openqg-bench/src/zyal_genome/theory_population.rs`, a re-clothed child should be constructed as a new `ProposalDoc` whose `theory` is the evolved descendant and whose `claims`, `obligations`, `unification`, and `evidence` come from the donor only as candidate text. Then run the exact `score_proposal` path from `proposer.rs` on the new document. No component from the donor scorecard may be copied.

The recomputation checklist is: `bind_modified_background` on the descendant; `run_veto_cascade` plus `adjudicate`; all `DerivedCertificate::check` values using the descendant's actual background where any input overlaps `h`, `omega_m`, `w0`, `wa`, `omega_b_h2`, `n_eff`, `sum_mnu`, `mu0`, `fr_*`, or `ndgp_*`; all `LimitWitness` residuals; all `NovelPredictionWitness` audits through `audit_novel_predictions`; all evidence materialization hashes; `UnificationClaim::shared_parameter_audit`; and the no-hidden-knob test. The `claim_graph_digest` must be recomputed after updating claim statements or obligations that mention numbers.

The graft should also require semantic symbol coverage. If a claim references `mu0`, `geff_over_g`, `omega_rc`, `f_R0`, or an alpha function, the descendant must either carry that symbol as a parameter with matching provenance or the binder must produce it. A claim about suppressed growth cannot be attached to a descendant whose bound background has `mu0=0` and `mg_family=none`. Conversely, a descendant with alpha-basis deviations needs claims and obligations explaining those alpha values; otherwise they are unprovenanced gravity modifications and should not receive novelty credit.

A safer alternative is proposal-seeded mutation. Instead of grafting after the fact, keep the proposal's `ProposalDoc` as the genotype and mutate only parameters named in its claim graph. After each mutation, run a "claim-preservation" pass that updates certificate inputs and novel witnesses from the new background, then scores the full proposal. This preserves ancestry: structure and fit co-evolve under the same oracle.

Another alternative is obligation-aware crossover. Recombine two individuals only if `recombination_compatible` can prove their claim graphs have non-conflicting obligations for shared symbols. When a child inherits a background coordinate from parent A and a claim from parent B, mark every affected obligation dirty and require re-verification before scoring. Dirty obligations earn zero until repaired; they must not be assumed valid.

The V6 engineering spec should introduce a `ReclothingCandidate` type with fields `donor_doc_sha`, `descendant_fingerprint`, `dirty_symbols`, `reverified_obligations`, and `invalidated_claims`. If `dirty_symbols` is nonempty and no corresponding reverified obligation exists, the candidate is disqualified. That makes re-clothing a search operator, not a score merger.

The ledger should also make failed re-clothing visible. A failed graft is valuable evidence: it tells the team which claims are too brittle under parameter optimization. Add `reclothing-ledger.jsonl` records with donor id, descendant id, dirty symbols, failed certificates, failed novel audits, and final score. The best V6 outcome may be not a perfect graft, but a map of which proposal structures survive movement along the data-fit valley.
