# 3. Proposed candidate: GSAU

## 3.1 Core idea

**GSAU = Geometric Spectral–Asymptotic Unification**

The theory is built from five principles:

1. **Geometry first:** particles and forces arise from a spectral geometry, not from arbitrary fields added by hand.
2. **Gauge unification:** at high energy, matter sits in a Pati–Salam/SO(10)-compatible structure.
3. **Quantum gravity by fixed point:** the UV completion is asymptotically safe, with finitely many relevant physical couplings.
4. **Low-energy survival:** below high scales, the theory becomes SM + GR + right-handed neutrinos + axion/PQ sector + optional dynamical dark energy.
5. **No anonymous parameters:** every parameter is a mass, coupling, mixing angle, symmetry-breaking scale, curvature coefficient, or vacuum-energy slope.

This candidate intentionally nests the best-tested theory. If new sectors decouple, it gives SM + GR + ΛCDM. If they do not, deviations appear in proton decay, neutrino observables, axion searches, dark-energy evolution, gravitational waves, black holes, fifth-force/equivalence-principle tests, or high-energy collider observables.

## 4. What GSAU predicts or forbids

1. **At low energy it reproduces SM + GR.** That is mandatory, not a bonus.

2. **No dimension-4 Lorentz or CPT violation.** In SME language, leading Lorentz/CPT-violating coefficients should be zero unless generated by controlled symmetry breaking. The SME data tables are ideal for this null test because they tabulate Lorentz/CPT coefficients across many experiments. ([Physics NMU][15])

3. **Gauge coupling unification with threshold corrections.**

[
g_1(M_U)=g_2(M_U)=g_3(M_U)+\Delta_{\rm threshold}.
]

4. **Proton decay is expected but may be highly suppressed.**

A rough operator form is

[
\mathcal L_{\Delta B}
\sim
\frac{g_U^2}{M_X^2}
(q q q \ell),
]

so

[
\tau_p \propto \frac{M_X^4}{g_U^4m_p^5}.
]

5. **Neutrino masses come from seesaw physics**, not arbitrary tiny Yukawas:

[
m_\nu\simeq -v^2Y_\nu M_N^{-1}Y_\nu^T.
]

6. **Strong CP is solved by the axion.** The axion is also a dark-matter candidate, testable by ADMX-like haloscope bounds and cosmological/astrophysical constraints. ADMX reports axion-search bounds in the micro-eV range in recent publications. ([UW Departments][16])

7. **Dark energy is either ΛCDM or a one-slope physical scalar.** The model reduces to ΛCDM when (s_{\rm DE}=0). If DESI/SN/weak-lensing data prefer evolution, (s_{\rm DE}\neq 0) is measurable.

8. **Gravitational-wave propagation has (c_T=c)** at leading low-energy order. Deviations from GR in ringdowns, stochastic backgrounds, or early-universe spectra are controlled by (\alpha_R,\alpha_C), not arbitrary waveform fudge factors.

9. **Flavor remains the weakest part.** GSAU gives a geometric home for Yukawas, but does not yet derive all fermion masses and mixings from first principles. This is an explicit gap, not hidden fitting.

# 5. How to test all contenders with one public error metric

For any model (M) with physical parameters (\theta_M), define

[
{\rm Score}(M)
==============

\min_{\theta_M}
\left[
-2\sum_D\ln \mathcal L_D(d_D|\theta_M)
+
\lambda_{\rm comp}N_{\rm eff}(M)
+
\lambda_{\rm prior}\Pi_{\rm physical}(\theta_M)
\right].
]

Where:

* (D) runs over public datasets.
* (N_{\rm eff}) is the number of independently fitted physical parameters.
* (\Pi_{\rm physical}) penalizes forbidden regions: unstable vacua, wrong symmetry breaking, superluminal modes, ghosts, unitarity violation, failed BBN, failed CMB, etc.
* The benchmark is

[
\Delta{\rm Score}
=================

{\rm Score}(M)-{\rm Score}({\rm SM+GR+\Lambda CDM}).
]

A model only wins if

[
\Delta{\rm Score}<0
]

after complexity penalties.

For Gaussian data,

[
-2\ln\mathcal L_D
=================

(d-m)^TC^{-1}(d-m)+\ln\det C.
]

For event-count data,

[
-2\ln\mathcal L_D
=================

-2\sum_i
\left[
n_i\ln\mu_i-\mu_i-\ln(n_i!)
\right].
]

For upper-limit searches,

[
-2\ln\mathcal L_D
\simeq
\left[
\frac{\max(0,q_{\rm pred}-q_{\rm lim})}{\sigma_{\rm lim}}
\right]^2.
]

For gravitational-wave posterior reuse,

[
\mathcal L_{\rm GW}(\theta_M)
\propto
\prod_e
\frac{1}{N_e}
\sum_{s=1}^{N_e}
\frac{p_M(h_e|\theta_M,\vartheta_{e,s})}
{p_{\rm ref}(h_e|\vartheta_{e,s})}.
]

This lets GSAU, string-inspired EFTs, asymptotic-safety EFTs, LQG phenomenology, GUTs, modified gravity, ΛCDM, (w_0w_a)CDM, and postquantum gravity all compete under the same evidence-like metric.

## Final judgment

The most promising established programs are **string/M-theory**, **asymptotic safety**, **holography**, **loop/spinfoam gravity**, **spectral/noncommutative geometry**, and **SO(10)/Pati–Salam GUTs**. None is complete and experimentally confirmed.

My best proposed theory is **GSAU** because it is the most data-compatible synthesis:

[

+
\text{Pati--Salam/SO(10) matter}
+
\text{seesaw neutrinos}
+
\text{PQ axion}
+

It is not gray-box. Its parameters are named physical quantities. It is complete enough to code, falsify, and score against public data. Its biggest weaknesses are also clear: flavor is not fully derived, the cosmological-constant problem is not solved from first principles, and the asymptotic-safety/spectral-geometry merger is a candidate construction rather than a demonstrated theorem.

That said, among models that could plausibly survive the full public-data battery, this is the strongest architecture I would test first.
