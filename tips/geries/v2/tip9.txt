## What the candidates are really trying to do

They are not actually delivering a completed theory of all physics. They are trying to pass the brief’s **pre-submission derivation gate** while staying clean under its anti-cheating rules. The brief makes three things non-negotiable:

1. **Derivation before adapter.** No row can be `predicted` unless the dossier already contains equations, assumptions, constants provenance, fixture mapping, units, and uncertainty propagation.

2. **No target leakage.** No fixture values, validation source IDs, benchmark feedback, reference-predictor outputs, lookup tables, fitted coefficients, neural regressors, or copied rows.

3. **Unsupported rows must be refused.** A weak theory that emits honest `not_implemented` rows is better than a high-coverage theory that fills CODATA, PDG, DESI, SPARC, JPL, EHT, or GWOSC rows by smuggling in measured values.

The strongest candidates all converge on the same architecture: **almost-commutative/spectral geometry for the Standard Model structure, anomaly/index constraints for charge and chirality, asymptotic safety or fixed-point RG for quantum-gravity completion, and exact low-energy GR/WEP/Lorentz-CPT null rules.** The spectral-action program is a real literature route because applying a spectral action to a noncommutative space associated with the Standard Model yields the Standard Model action coupled to gravity, and later work incorporates neutrino mixing/seesaw structure. ([arXiv][1]) Asymptotic safety is also a real quantum-gravity program, but it is still a framework with unresolved matter-coupling and low-energy extraction issues, not a completed numerical derivation of all constants. ([arXiv][2])

## 1. Core theory

**IACSAU-1** says:

> Low-energy physics is the infrared limit of a Lorentzian almost-commutative spectral geometry whose finite internal algebra fixes the Standard Model representation structure; whose index/anomaly constraints fix charge quantization and chirality; whose gravitational sector lies on an asymptotically safe RG trajectory; and whose predictions are separated into law-derived, state-conditioned, and unsupported rows.

The primitive object is

[

(M,g,\mathcal C;\mathcal A,\mathcal H,D,J,\Gamma;\Gamma_k,\mathcal L_{\rm state}),

with

[
\mathcal A=C^\infty(M)\otimes\mathcal A_F,

\mathcal A_F=\mathbb C\oplus\mathbb H\oplus M_3(\mathbb C).

The fluctuated Dirac operator is

[
D_A=D+A+JAJ^{-1},

S_{\rm IACSAU}
==============

{\rm Tr},f(D_A^2/\Lambda_s^2)

\Gamma^{\rm AS}*{k\to0}[g,\Phi*{\rm SM}]

The first two terms are the spectral-action/fermion sector. The fifth is not a physics fudge factor; it is a bookkeeping ledger for source-specific boundary/initial conditions that must not be confused with derived universal constants.

This choice beats string/F-theory for this specific benchmark not because string theory is less ambitious, but because the benchmark rewards **auditable fixture-independent extraction**. String compactifications can embed gauge and gravitational interactions and reproduce Standard-Model-like structures, but selecting a unique compactification and deriving the exact low-energy constants remains a major obstacle for a no-leakage prediction adapter. ## 3. Low-energy laws and strict predictions

IACSAU-1 only emits strict predictions for rows that follow from exact low-energy symmetry or limit statements.

### `microscope_*`: WEP null rows

Matter couples universally to the same metric in the IR Einstein-frame limit:

[
S_m[\psi_i,g_{\mu\nu},A_\mu,\ldots],
]

so the free-fall acceleration is composition-independent at tree level:

[
\eta_{AB}
=========

# 2\frac{|a_A-a_B|}{|a_A+a_B|}

0.

]

This maps to MICROSCOPE-style Eötvös rows as an exact zero prediction. MICROSCOPE is a real final-result WEP data surface, testing the equivalence principle in terms of the Eötvös ratio at the (10^{-15}) scale. ([APS Physics][6])

### `ligogr_*`: LIGO/Virgo/KAGRA GR null-test rows

The low-energy gravitational action reduces to Einstein-Hilbert plus suppressed higher-curvature terms:

[
S_g
===

\frac{1}{16\pi G_{\rm eff}}
\int d^4x\sqrt{-g}(R-2\Lambda_{\rm eff})
+
O(R^2/\Lambda_s^2).
]

For fixture rows whose observable is a dimensionless GR-deviation parameter in the IR domain,

[
\Delta_{\rm GR}=0.
]

This is valid for GR-null rows, not for GWOSC catalog masses or event-specific source parameters. LIGO/Virgo/KAGRA tests of GR with GWTC-3 are a real strong-field/dynamical-gravity test surface. ([arXiv][7])

### `sme_*`: Lorentz/CPT null rows

The fundamental low-energy vacuum is locally Lorentz invariant and CPT invariant. Therefore Standard-Model Extension coefficients for fundamental Lorentz/CPT violation vanish:

[
a_\mu=b_\mu=c_{\mu\nu}=d_{\mu\nu}=H_{\mu\nu}=\cdots=0.
]

This can emit `sme_*` rows only when the observable shape is a coefficient value or coefficient magnitude where zero is a legitimate strict prediction. The SME tables are a real curated source of Lorentz/CPT coefficient bounds and measurements. ([arXiv][8])
