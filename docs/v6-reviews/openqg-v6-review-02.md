# Gameability red-team of V6.1

V6.1 closes the obvious V5/V6 faucets: no diagonal likelihood hiding, no free background drift, no no-witness novelty, no fabricated witness above three times tolerance, no decorative unification scaffold over a drifted background. The remaining attacks are subtler. They mostly exploit mismatches between the symbolic layer, the compressed forward model, and the proposer loop.

Attack 1: exploit the CMB calibration derivative. `crates/openqg-core/src/cosmology/forward.rs::BackgroundForwardModel::compute` applies constant offsets to `cmb_R` and `cmb_lA`. A proposal can search for backgrounds where the raw fitting formula's residual relative to Boltzmann truth is not constant. The anchor test only pins one point. The V6 exploit used `h` drift to harvest the `lA` fitting-formula bias; V7 should assume the next exploit uses local slope or curvature in `(h, omega_m, omega_b_h2, n_eff, w0, wa)`.

Attack 2: action-term laundering. `crates/openqg-core/src/theory/vetoes.rs::adjudicate` looks for substrings like `quintessence`, `scalar`, `dark_energy`, `k_essence`, `galileon`, `drag`, `coupling`, `gauss`, or `horndeski`. This is both underinclusive and overinclusive. A fake term named `quintessence_decoration` can satisfy dynamic dark-energy structure, while a bound nDGP or f(R) modification is not required to carry any corresponding term. The survivor demonstrates this: `run-data/v6-chunk-6-white-paper.json` has a certified nDGP growth parameter but only GR plus cosmological-constant terms.

Attack 3: direct-symbol binding as a back door. `crates/openqg-core/src/theory/binding.rs::bind_modified_background` copies `Fundamental` parameters whose symbols are exactly `mu0`, `ndgp_omega_rc`, `fr_log10_fr0`, or `fr_n` into the background when no relation owns the field. `crates/openqg-core/src/theory/scorecard.rs::free_dof` treats `Provenance::Fundamental` as free of cost. That means a proposer can label a new MG amplitude as fundamental and avoid both `free_dof` and derivation rigor requirements, unless a separate registry of admitted fundamental constants exists. The test `binding.rs::direct_fundamental_omega_rc_binds_without_relation` makes this behavior explicit.

Attack 4: fit-set witness drip. V6.1 caps fit-set witnesses at 0.25, but 0.25 times the 20-point novelty component is still 5 points for a statement about data already used in the likelihood. In `scorecard.rs::score_with_observables`, one `ComputedHonestDistinct` audit with `in_fit_set` earns that tier. A campaign can still turn fitted residual explanations into a small reward. For a rubric that should be hostile to self-validation, fit-set novelty should be zero and recorded as explanatory metadata only.

Attack 5: engine-refreshed tier stacking via lineages. The code assigns a single tier, not additive stacking, so `-rc-rc-rc` does not multiply points directly. But re-clothing remains a search operator that repeatedly carries donor claims into new backgrounds. `crates/openqg-bench/src/zyal_genome/theory_population.rs::reclothe_candidate` refreshes witnesses and marks them `refreshed_by_engine`. The cap is good. The laundering risk is now selection pressure: the operator discovers which donor structures survive the current oracle and repackages them at high frequency.

Attack 6: measurement floor evasion through non-fit observables. `binding.rs::audit_novel_predictions_with_context` applies the measurement-sigma floor only when the canonical witness observable appears in `fit_observables`. An out-of-fit-set witness can provide a tiny `min_detectable` and, because honesty tolerance is `min(min_detectable, 1 percent of computed value)`, force absurd precision. That can also fabricate-kill proposals too harshly, but a model that learns the exact computed value can claim out-of-fit distinctness at an artificial resolution.

Attack 7: memory monoculture. `crates/openqg-bench/src/zyal_genome/proposer_memory.rs::assemble_memory` gates top-scorers through `physics_kills`, which is good, but it does not require current scorecard re-evaluation with current observables, covariance, novelty context, or V6.1 rescore metadata. It can still promote a proposal that passes physics but earned its score under an older novelty or data-fit rule.

Attack 8: oracle-repair teaching. `proposer_router.rs::propose_one_sample` tells the LLM: for fabricated novel predictions, the kill message contains the engine-computed value and the model should set the witness to exactly that. That makes replay honest but also collapses proposal learning into copying the forward model. V7 should split numerical repair from creative proposal generation and ledger both separately.

Attack 9: robustness free lunch. `scorecard.rs` maps negative `generalization_gap` to perfect robustness because it uses `max(0.0)` inside the denominator. A candidate can luck into heldout better than train and always receive 13/13. With alternating splits over the same admitted data, this is not an adversarial robustness judge.

Attack 10: compressed-data overfitting with no out-of-fit registry. The campaign admits Planck distance priors, BAO blocks, and growth/S8-style observables, then lets proposals cite the same observables as witnesses. Until there is a timestamped prediction registry outside the fit set, "novelty" remains partially gameable even after V6.1.
