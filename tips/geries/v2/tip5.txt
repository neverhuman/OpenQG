# 1. Candidate: MCSFP-1

## 1.1 Core idea

MCSFP-1 combines five structures:

1. **Lorentzian causal spectral geometry** for the ontology of spacetime and internal degrees of freedom.
2. **Noncommutative/spectral-action internal geometry** for the Standard Model gauge structure, Higgs sector, chiral fermions, and Pati–Salam/SO(10)-like matter organization.
3. **Asymptotic-safety fixed-point dynamics** for quantum gravity plus matter couplings.
4. **Modular automorphic flavor sector** to turn the arbitrary Yukawa/flavor matrices of older candidates into functions of a finite modular datum.
5. **Strict OpenQG extraction discipline**: emit numbers only where derivations are fixture-independent; refuse everything else.

The spectral-action basis is grounded in the noncommutative-geometry program in which the Standard Model with neutrino mixing and gravity emerges from a product of four-dimensional spacetime with a finite noncommutative space; the Pati–Salam extension follows from relaxing the first-order condition and yields (SU(2)_R\times SU(2)_L\times SU(4))-type structure with quarks and leptons unified in four colors. ([arXiv][12])

The asymptotic-safety piece supplies the fixed-point condition: a UV-complete theory must approach a finite-dimensional critical surface of the functional RG flow. This is why MCSFP-1 treats many low-energy numbers as **boundary-value outputs of a UV fixed-point problem**, not as fitted constants. Prior asymptotic-safety work has used this logic to constrain Higgs-sector and top-Yukawa trajectories, although those predictions are assumption-sensitive. ([arXiv][13])

The modular-flavor piece is the new hardening step. Instead of free Yukawa matrices (Y_u,Y_d,Y_e,Y_\nu), MCSFP-1 makes them modular forms evaluated at a modulus (\tau_\star), with (\tau_\star) selected by a spectral determinant principle. Modular flavor models are a known framework in which Yukawa couplings and lepton mixing are constrained by finite modular symmetries; MCSFP-1 embeds that idea into the spectral/fixed-point action rather than using it as a phenomenological fit. ([arXiv][14])
