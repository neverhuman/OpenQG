## Bottom line

The strongest *existing* contenders are not single “finished theories.” They are research programs that each solve part of the unification problem: string/M-theory is strongest on quantum gravity + gauge/matter in principle; asymptotic safety is strongest as a 4D quantum-field-theoretic UV-completion program; loop/spinfoam gravity and causal dynamical triangulations are strongest on background-independent/nonperturbative spacetime; noncommutative geometry is unusually good at turning the Standard Model’s gauge/Higgs structure into geometry; SO(10)-type GUTs remain the cleanest particle-unification framework; and quantum-information/holographic approaches are the sharpest route into emergent spacetime and black-hole information.

My best falsifiable proposal is a **spectral–asymptotically safe axion–seesaw theory**: Standard Model geometry from a noncommutative/spectral action, quantum gravity from an asymptotically safe metric RG fixed point, three right-handed neutrinos for the observed neutrino masses, and a Peccei–Quinn axion sector for strong CP and dark matter. It is deliberately conservative: it reduces to **SM + GR + ΛCDM** wherever those are already extremely successful, while adding only named, physical parameters. The incomplete part is the same incomplete part shared by asymptotic-safety and spectral-action programs: a fully proven nonperturbative Lorentzian fixed point and a unique derivation of all Yukawa/flavor data.

## 1. What counts as “promising” here

I would rank a unified theory by five criteria:

1. **Low-energy recovery:** exactly reproduces GR, the Standard Model, neutrino oscillations, cosmology, and precision tests.
2. **UV completion:** no uncontrolled infinities at arbitrarily short distances.
3. **Predictivity:** few parameters, and all parameters have physical names: masses, charges, vacuum density, symmetry-breaking scales, curvature couplings, Yukawa matrices, mixing angles.
4. **Calculability:** produces numbers for collider rates, cosmology, gravitational waves, black holes, equivalence-principle tests, and dark-matter searches.
5. **Falsifiability with open data:** can be scored against public datasets rather than only aesthetic arguments.

The current baseline to beat is not “nothing”: it is **GR + Standard Model + ΛCDM as effective field theories**. A recent broad review of fundamental physics emphasizes that this baseline is powerful but leaves open dark matter, neutrino masses, baryogenesis, dark energy/cosmological constant, quantum gravity, and naturalness questions. ([arXiv][1]) Planck’s final full-mission CMB analysis found strong consistency with flat six-parameter ΛCDM, while DESI DR2 has reported robust low-redshift trends that can be captured by two-parameter evolving dark-energy extensions, so cosmology is now one of the best places to stress-test any unified model. ([arXiv][2])

## 3. My proposed unified theory: spectral–asymptotically safe axion–seesaw unification

I will call the model **SASAS**: **S**pectral **A**symptotic-**S**afety with **A**xion–**S**eesaw matter.

It is not a claim that the open problem is “solved.” It is a complete mathematical candidate in the same sense that a modern BSM/QG proposal is complete: it specifies fields, symmetries, action, RG completion principle, observables, and falsification targets. The conjectural step is the existence and uniqueness of the required asymptotically safe Lorentzian fixed point.

### 3.1 Fundamental ingredients

Spacetime is a four-dimensional globally hyperbolic spin manifold ((M,g_{\mu\nu})), with quantum fields defined by a spectral triple:

[
\mathcal A = C^\infty(M)\otimes \mathcal A_F,
\qquad
\mathcal A_F=\mathbb C\oplus \mathbb H\oplus M_3(\mathbb C),
]

[
\mathcal H=L^2(M,S)\otimes \mathcal H_F,
\qquad
D = \slashed{D}_g\otimes 1+\gamma_5\otimes D_F .
]

The inner fluctuations of the Dirac operator,

[
D_A=D+A+JAJ^{-1},
]

generate the Standard Model gauge and Higgs fields. The physical gauge group is

[
G_{\rm SM}=\frac{SU(3)_c\times SU(2)_L\times U(1)_Y}{\mathbb Z_6}.
]

Matter consists of the Standard Model fermions plus three right-handed neutrinos (N_i), a Higgs doublet (H), and one complex Peccei–Quinn scalar (\Phi). After PQ breaking,

[
\Phi(x)=\frac{f_a+s(x)}{\sqrt{2}}e^{ia(x)/f_a},
]

where (a) is the axion and (f_a) is the PQ symmetry-breaking scale.

## 4. What this model explains, and what it still does not

**Explains or organizes cleanly:**

* GR as the low-energy spin-2 sector.
* Standard Model gauge/Higgs structure from spectral geometry.
* Neutrino masses through a named seesaw sector.
* Strong CP through the axion.
* Dark matter through axion misalignment and/or topological defects, depending on PQ cosmology.
* Baryogenesis through thermal or nonthermal leptogenesis from heavy (N_i).
* Quantum gravity through an asymptotically safe fixed point, if the fixed point exists.

**Open gaps:**

* It does not yet derive all Yukawa matrices uniquely. The entries have physical meaning, but they are still measured inputs unless the finite spectral triple plus UV fixed point fixes them.
* The Lorentzian nonperturbative fixed point is a conjecture, not a theorem.
* The (C^2) curvature term must be handled by the asymptotically safe nonperturbative propagator or by a ghost-free form-factor completion; a naive perturbative higher-derivative theory has ghost concerns.
* Dark energy is (\rho_\Lambda) in the minimal model. Given DESI DR2 hints, a running-vacuum deformation should be tested, but I would not hard-wire evolving dark energy until independent confirmation. DESI itself reports evidence for dynamical dark-energy behavior in combined analyses, but this remains a model-comparison frontier rather than settled physics. ([DESI][16])

## 5. Concrete predictions and falsifiers

The model should be penalized or rejected if any of the following occurs:

1. **No viable asymptotic-safety fixed point** exists for gravity plus this matter content.
2. **Axion window closes** for the PQ charge assignment used: ADMX/haloscope, helioscope, stellar, supernova, CMB isocurvature, and direct dark-matter constraints exclude the required (f_a).
3. **Neutrinos are proven purely Dirac** with no viable Majorana/seesaw interpretation and no acceptable modification.
4. **Equivalence principle violation or Lorentz violation** is found at levels incompatible with the predicted suppressed operators.
5. **Proton decay constraints** rule out the optional SO(10)/GUT embedding. The non-GUT spectral version need not predict proton decay.
6. **CMB + LSS + SN + BAO** reject Λ plus axion dark matter and also reject the small running-vacuum deformation derived from RG flow.
7. **Precision collider/flavor data** require new low-energy operators not generated by the spectral/asymptotic-safe operator basis.

## 6. Error metric for comparing all contenders

For a candidate theory (T) with physical parameter vector (\theta_T), define

[
\chi^2_T(\theta_T)
==================

\sum_d
\left[
\mathbf y_d-\mathbf F_d(\theta_T)
\right]^T
C_d^{-1}
\left[
\mathbf y_d-\mathbf F_d(\theta_T)
\right]
+
\sum_b\chi^2_{b,\rm bounds}
+
\sum_e\chi^2_{e,\rm events}.
]

Use likelihoods where available:

[
-2\ln \mathcal L_T
==================

\chi^2_T+\text{constants}.
]

For model comparison, use Bayesian evidence

[
Z_T=\int d\theta_T,\mathcal L(D|\theta_T,T),\pi(\theta_T|T),
]

or a practical penalized score

[
\mathcal S_T
============

-2\ln \mathcal L(D|\hat\theta_T,T)
+
2N_{\rm phys}
+
P_{\rm opaque}
+
P_{\rm inconsistency}.
]

Here (N_{\rm phys}) counts only physically named free parameters, (P_{\rm opaque}) heavily penalizes uninterpretable gray-box functions or arbitrary fitted form factors, and (P_{\rm inconsistency}) penalizes anomalies, ghosts, unitarity failures, missing continuum limits, or unproven recovery of GR/SM in tested regimes.

## 9. The strongest near-term tests

The most decisive public-data tests over the next few years are:

1. **DESI + SN + CMB:** decide whether Λ is enough or whether evolving dark energy is required.
2. **CMB/LSS axion bounds:** constrain (f_a), axion isocurvature, and axion fraction of dark matter.
3. **GW ringdowns:** test strong-field GR and possible quantum-gravity corrections.
4. **Neutrinoless double beta decay:** test Majorana neutrinos and the seesaw.
5. **Axion haloscopes/helioscopes:** directly attack the PQ sector.
6. **Proton decay:** if the spectral model is embedded into SO(10), this becomes a sharp falsifier.
7. **Equivalence-principle and inverse-square-law tests:** constrain hidden scalar/curvature sectors.
8. **Collider reinterpretation:** test whether the PQ radial mode, heavy neutrinos, or threshold remnants are allowed.

## Final assessment

The best current path is **not** a purely new algebraic model with many fitted coefficients. The most promising route is a conservative synthesis:

[
\boxed{
\text{Spectral Standard Model geometry}
+
\text{asymptotically safe quantum gravity}
+
\text{seesaw neutrinos}
+
\text{PQ axion dark matter}
+
\text{Λ or tightly constrained RG vacuum}
}
]

This gives a mathematically specified action, named physical parameters, direct recovery of known physics, and a public-data testing path. Its biggest weakness is not data fit; it will fit today’s data well because it reduces to the tested SM/GR/ΛCDM regime. Its biggest weakness is **derivational uniqueness**: proving the UV fixed point, fixing flavor, and deriving the finite spectral triple and PQ sector without hand selection. Those are exactly the gaps the next round of theory and open-data scoring should target.
