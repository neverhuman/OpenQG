## What the uploaded brief makes non-negotiable

The benchmark is not asking for a theory that sounds unified. It is asking for a **runnable prediction artifact** with one row per observable, where every numeric `predicted` value has a derivation independent of the fixture target value. It explicitly forbids neural regressors, empirical surrogates, reference-predictor reuse, copied fixture values, and post-hoc calibration to validation rows. It also states that unsupported rows are acceptable and should reduce coverage rather than become dishonest numeric predictions. 

That changes the problem. Many “unified theories” can explain broad structures, but they cannot derive, without source-specific calibration, values like an individual binary black-hole mass in GWOSC, a SPARC galaxy’s rotation velocity, a JPL orbital element, or the EHT angular diameter of M87*. Those are partly lawlike, but also depend on historical boundary conditions, source morphology, masses, distances, selection effects, and observational pipelines.

So the decisive criterion is not “can the theory gesture at everything?” It is:

**Can it emit non-empirical, source-backed rows across the OpenQG observable families without cheating?**

The universal fixture families in the brief include CODATA constants, PDG particles, electroweak HEPData rows, NuFIT neutrino summaries, DESI/BOSS BAO, Planck, Pantheon+, GWOSC compact binaries, LIGO GR null tests, EHT, MICROSCOPE WEP, SME Lorentz/CPT, NIST ASD atomic spectra, JPL Horizons, and SPARC rotation curves.  These are current, serious data surfaces: CODATA/NIST maintains recommended constants, PDG maintains the particle-data review, NuFIT 6.0 updates global neutrino fits, DESI Y1 reports BAO over millions of objects, Planck gives final CMB parameter constraints, Pantheon+ compiles supernova distance data, LVK catalogs compact-binary events, EHT reports black-hole image scales, and SPARC collects galaxy rotation curves. ## The winning hardened theory: SAGE-0

**Name:** SAGE-0
**Long name:** Spectral–Asymptotic Gauge-gravity EFT
**Status:** candidate artifact architecture, not a proven final theory
**Core claim:** the strongest currently honest unification artifact should combine:

4. **Strict abstention rules** for fixture values that require environmental, historical, or target-source calibration.

### Postulates

1. **Kinematic postulate.**
   The low-energy particle content is represented by an almost-commutative spectral geometry of the rough form
   [
   C^\infty(M)\otimes A_F,
   ]
   where (A_F) encodes the Standard Model internal algebra, including chiral fermions and neutrino-sector structure.

2. **Action postulate.**
   Dynamics are generated by a spectral action plus fermionic action:
   [
   S = \mathrm{Tr}, f(D^2/\Lambda^2) + \langle J\Psi,D\Psi\rangle,
   ]
   whose expansion yields Einstein–Hilbert gravity, Standard Model gauge terms, scalar/Higgs terms, and higher-curvature corrections.

3. **UV postulate.**
   The spectral-action EFT is embedded in an asymptotically safe gravity–matter RG flow. This is what prevents the theory from being only a low-energy effective description.

4. **IR postulate.**
   At scales probed by MICROSCOPE, LIGO/Virgo/KAGRA tests of GR, ordinary atomic physics, and low-energy Standard Model tests, the leading operational theory is Lorentz/CPT-invariant SM + metric GR.

5. **Benchmark honesty postulate.**
   SAGE-0 emits a numeric prediction only when the observable row is a direct consequence of the above structure and does not require target-source calibration. Everything else is `not_implemented` until a real derivation exists.

This is not “curve fitting plus metaphysics.” It is a constrained artifact: **predict null deviations where the theory’s IR limit demands null deviations; abstain where the row encodes contingent data.**

## Final two: why SAGE-0 beats string/holographic compactification

### Finalist A: SAGE-0

**Strengths**

SAGE-0 has a clean path to non-leaking predictions for GR null tests, WEP null tests, and possibly Lorentz/CPT null-coefficient rows. It has an explicit representation of Standard Model gauge/matter structure through spectral geometry, and a plausible UV-completion story through asymptotic safety. It is also naturally conservative: it does not pretend to derive the measured electron mass, (\alpha), galaxy rotation curves, binary black-hole component masses, or cosmological distances unless a real derivation exists.

**Weaknesses**

It still does not derive most Standard Model numerical constants. It does not derive the cosmological initial conditions behind DESI, Planck, Pantheon+, BOSS/eBOSS, or individual galaxy/source observables. It has formal issues to harden, especially Lorentzian spectral geometry, exact RG completion, and predictive parameter reduction.

### Finalist B: string/M-theory compactification + holography

**Strengths**

It has the deepest traditional claim to quantum-gravity unification. It naturally includes gravity, gauge sectors, extended objects, dualities, black-hole entropy, and holographic consistency. String compactifications can generate rich low-energy gauge structures, and holography is one of the clearest realized links between quantum theory and gravity. ([arXiv][3])

**Weaknesses**

For this benchmark, the problem is the vacuum/moduli/landscape layer. If the adapter chooses a compactification, moduli stabilization, fluxes, brane data, or cosmological background using fixture-like information, the prediction is contaminated. If it does not choose them, it cannot emit many numbers. Therefore string/holographic compactification may be more ambitious as a theory of nature, but **less safe as a strict OpenQG artifact today**.

**Winner:** SAGE-0.

## Why I reject the tempting “100% coverage” route

A model could get apparent high coverage by doing any of the following:

* copying CODATA, PDG, Planck, DESI, Pantheon+, GWOSC, EHT, SPARC, or JPL values;
* using the calibrated `sm-gr-lcdm-eft-calibrated` reference predictor;
* fitting constants to benchmark rows;
* treating source-specific astrophysical data as if it were derived from first principles;
* using empirical surrogates for galaxy curves, atomic spectra, or cosmological distances.

The uploaded brief explicitly redlines those shortcuts.  So a true “hardened” answer must prefer low honest coverage over high contaminated coverage.
