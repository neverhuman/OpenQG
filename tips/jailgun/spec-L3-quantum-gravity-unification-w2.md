## 1. Data sources and data governance

The project’s stated source surface already points in the right direction: cosmology fixtures such as `data/fixtures/cosmology/tier0-combined.jsonl`, `growth-rsd.jsonl`, and `sh0es-h0.jsonl`; theory-data registries; and universal source families including DESI/BAO, BOSS/eBOSS, Planck, Pantheon+, GWOSC, LIGO GR null tests, MICROSCOPE, SME, EHT, NIST, PDG, NuFIT, JPL Horizons, and SPARC. The strict-theory operating contract visible in the indexed material says candidates must emit exactly one row per observable and use `predicted` only when the value is fixture-independent, with unsupported rows marked `not_implemented` or `invalid_domain`. That principle is correct and should be promoted from documentation into executable schema enforcement.

The missing piece is hierarchy and provenance pressure. Not every datum is equally admissible for every claim. SH0ES-H0 is a tension diagnostic, not a standalone discovery lane. Planck distance priors are not a substitute for a Boltzmann likelihood when the theory changes recombination, early energy density, primordial spectra, lensing, neutrino physics, or sound-horizon calibration. DESI/Y1 or DR-style BAO compressed products can score late-time distance models, but EDE or interacting dark-sector models need perturbations and sound-horizon consistency. RSD and weak-lensing products are not decorative: they are where many background-only dark-energy stories die.

Concrete fix: add a `data_product.yml` next to each fixture/source pack with fields: `observable_family`, `valid_model_class`, `required_forward_fidelity`, `covariance_block_id`, `nuisance_parameters`, `blinding_status`, `source_release`, `citation`, `used_for_calibration_allowed`, and `used_for_validation_allowed`. The engine must reject a candidate whose theory class exceeds the model validity of the data product. A coupled-dark-sector model cannot score on background distances alone unless the artifact says “background-only diagnostic; not evidence for coupled-DE.”

Verification should use three lanes. First, a data-order lane shuffles rows and proves identical scores. Second, a covariance lane mutates off-diagonal covariance and proves the likelihood changes. Third, a leakage lane injects fixture values into a fake theory adapter and verifies vetoes fire at row level and run level. The review artifact should name the exact source-pack hashes and fixture locks used, never “latest data” without a release ID.

## 2. Forward-model fidelity

The architectural claim described in the request is a forward model for background plus growth with equations, scoring, previews, and honest limitations: no Boltzmann CMB, diagonal-vs-covariance concerns, analytic-only unification, and no scoreable EDE/coupled-DE. That honesty is valuable, but the engine must turn it into machine-enforced validity domains.

For `crates/openqg-core/src/cosmology/background.rs`, require a single normalized interface for `E(z)`, comoving distance, angular-diameter distance, luminosity distance, sound-horizon handling, and derived BAO quantities. The model must declare whether `r_d` is fixed, externally profiled, internally predicted, or invalid. For `growth.rs`, do not allow a growth-index shortcut to masquerade as modified-gravity perturbation theory. Keep fast `γ` growth as a diagnostic, but add a validity flag that refuses scale-dependent modified gravity, coupled-DE drag, clustering dark energy, or EDE unless a perturbation module exists. For `forward.rs`, every output vector should carry derivatives, units, data-product mapping, and validity flags so `likelihood.rs` can refuse invalid rows instead of scoring garbage.

Add four fidelity levels. `compressed_background` handles LCDM, wCDM, w0wa with fixed early physics and compressed BAO/SN/H0 diagnostics. `background_growth_cov` adds RSD, `fσ8`, growth ODEs, and covariance. `boltzmann_table` uses precomputed CLASS/CAMB-like grids or external validated tables for CMB/lensing-sensitive candidates. `full_likelihood_external` delegates to external likelihood engines while preserving receipts. A candidate must declare its required level; the host computes the minimum valid level from theory metadata and data-product metadata. If the declared level is lower, reject or downgrade to “exploratory only.”

EDE and coupled dark energy are the immediate test cases. EDE changes the sound horizon and CMB damping/lensing constraints; coupled-DE changes perturbations and may create instabilities. A valid engine must include stability checks: positive effective sound speed where applicable, no ghost/gradient instability in the declared EFT, conserved total stress-energy, bounded early density fraction, and priors defined before data scoring. A running-vacuum model must specify whether `G` runs, whether matter is conserved, and how Bianchi identities are satisfied.

## 3. Statistical rigor and model selection

The theory league should be a referee, not a leaderboard. It needs to answer different questions separately:

1. Does the model fit the scored data better? Use χ²/log-likelihood with covariance and nuisance profiling.
2. Does the improvement survive complexity penalties? Use ΔAIC and ΔBIC with explicit effective parameter count.
3. Is there actual Bayesian support? Use evidence or a defensible approximation with registered priors and prior-volume sensitivity.
4. Does it predict held-out data? Use pre-registered train/validation splits and predictive log score.
5. Does it earn unification credit? Only if parameters or structural claims are derived independently of target fixtures.

Do not collapse these into `openqg_score`. Keep `fit_score`, `predictive_score`, `evidence_score`, `strict_theory_score`, `coverage_score`, and `vetoes` as separate fields. A flexible w0wa model may improve χ² and still lose BIC/evidence. A partial strict theory may have low coverage but high honesty. A null GR/LCDM limit may be an anchor but should not be advertised as frontier discovery.

The likelihood implementation must include log-normalization and covariance determinants where comparing data subsets or covariance choices. Nuisance parameters should be profiled or marginalized in a receipt-visible way. For SN, include covariance and nuisance handling; for BAO, respect consensus covariance and anisotropic/isotropic distinctions; for RSD/lensing, handle cross-correlations or explicitly mark a product independent only if the source supports that assumption.

The most important adversarial tests are simple. Create two candidates with the same functional form: one with parameters fit on the validation rows, one with parameters fixed from an allowed training split. The former must lose strict theory credit. Create a model with one extra parameter that improves χ² by less than 2; AIC/BIC should expose it. Create a diagonalized covariance copy of a correlated fixture; scores must change and the receipt must label the run non-production.

## 4. Theory coverage and the unified-physics target worth chasing

Operationally, “the unified theory most worthy of attention” cannot mean “the most beautiful Planck-scale story.” It must mean: a proposal derives a low-energy action or effective prediction map; reduces to SM/GR/LCDM where required; emits falsifiable predictions or hard priors before seeing validation data; carries uncertainty and domain validity; and loses to simpler baselines when its extra freedom is not predictive. By that definition, most pure quantum-gravity programs should not enter the league as theories. String landscape, loop quantum gravity, causal dynamical triangulations, asymptotic safety, and causal sets may be intellectually important, but they are not scoreable unless they export low-energy handles: a running vacuum/dark-sector EFT, a primordial spectrum feature, a bounce cutoff, a tensor spectrum, non-Gaussianity, Lorentz-violation coefficients, equivalence-principle deviations, modified propagation, or a stochastic gravitational-wave signature.

The strongest handles today are:

- **Primordial perturbations:** tensor-to-scalar ratio `r`, tensor tilt, scalar tilt/running, non-Gaussianity, isocurvature, oscillatory/step features, low-ℓ anomalies, and reheating priors. This is closest to early-universe quantum gravity, but discoveries are hard; current BICEP/Keck-style constraints are still upper-limit dominated, with Simons Observatory and later CMB-S4 improving sensitivity.
- **Dark-sector dynamics:** BAO/SN/H0 distances plus RSD, weak lensing, CMB lensing, and growth consistency. DESI-era hints of time-varying dark energy are not a QG discovery, but they are the best near-term stress test of vacuum energy physics. The fatal danger is background-only over-interpretation.
- **Stochastic gravitational-wave backgrounds:** PTA nanohertz signals, LVK stochastic searches, and future LISA/ET bands can test cosmic strings, phase transitions, primordial black holes, and nonstandard early-universe histories. These are promising but astrophysical foregrounds and model degeneracies are severe.
- **Equivalence-principle and Lorentz/CPT tests:** MICROSCOPE and SME bounds are exceptionally clean null constraints. They are powerful vetoes on theories, but low discovery probability and often weak model discrimination.

The single 2–5 year scoreable target I would prioritize is **a covariant dark-sector / running-vacuum EFT lane constrained jointly by distances and growth**, with a primordial-signature sidecar. This is not a claim that asymptotic safety or any running-vacuum program is correct. It is the best ratio of fundamental significance to attainable scoreability. Vacuum energy is central to unification; DESI/Euclid/LSST/Roman/ACT/SPT/Planck-derived products can move belief within years; and the engine can falsify many stories by requiring growth, lensing, covariance, and stability consistency. Among QG-adjacent programs, asymptotic-safety/running-coupling style low-energy exports currently offer the most scoreable handle because they can be encoded as constrained running of `Λ`, `G`, or effective dark-sector functions. But the bar must be high: without derived parameter values or registered priors, this is a phenomenological EFT lane, not a confirmed quantum-gravity theory.

Concrete program:

1. Define theory families: LCDM anchor; wCDM; w0waCDM; running-vacuum model with conservation choice declared; coupled-DE with interaction `Q` and stability constraints; scalar-field quintessence with potential classes; EDE as early-physics candidate; and modified-gravity slip/growth parameterizations.
2. Define observables: BAO `D_M/r_d`, `D_H/r_d`, `D_V/r_d`; SN distance moduli; H0 diagnostic; CMB distance priors only where valid; RSD `fσ8`; weak-lensing `S8`/compressed shear; CMB lensing amplitude; and optional GW standard siren diagnostics as data mature.
3. Define score outputs: background-only diagnostic; growth-consistent likelihood; holdout predictive score; AIC/BIC/evidence; strict theory credit only for derived values or pre-registered priors.
4. Define falsifiers: a model is demoted if it fits distances but fails growth/lensing, requires unstable perturbations, shifts `r_d` without a valid early-universe module, violates local gravity constraints, or wins only under diagonal covariance.
5. Define belief movement: a serious signal requires repeated improvement over LCDM across independent SN choices and BAO releases, non-degraded growth/lensing, stable posterior under prior variations, and improvement in predictive held-out data, not just Δχ² on the same compressed products.

## 5. ZYAL multi-agent design

The orchestration claim “LLM proposes; deterministic host judges” is exactly right. The current hardening target is to make that impossible to bypass. Rust should own policy, config schemas, tar validation, run contracts, route normalization, selected-path allowlists, receipts, and final acceptance. TypeScript should render dashboards, browser surfaces, and review ergonomics. LLMs can write proposals, critiques, and repair candidates; they cannot decide that evidence is sufficient.

The accessible ZYAL packets expose two specific failure patterns worth fixing immediately. First, hard-stage repair packets show route-tier ambiguity: `top20_pct` observed where validation expects `top20_pct_only`. That must become a typed route policy with raw and normalized fields, not a string convention spread across prompts. Second, several stage-review packets emphasize missing required evidence contents, root-cause labels, failure penalties, or lineage. ZYAL should fail closed when a stage names `run-events.jsonl`, `stage-ledger.jsonl`, and stage evaluation events but does not materialize the exact record IDs or hashes used.

Recommended agents: `theory_proposer`, `derivation_auditor`, `data_steward`, `likelihood_referee`, `physics_adversary`, `software_repro_auditor`, and `editor`. Only the deterministic host may promote. Each agent output should be a typed artifact with: input receipt hash, prompt/template hash, model/provider identifier or fakeable boundary marker, source-card IDs, claims, evidence refs, veto checks, and downstream repair target. Failure slicing must identify the smallest falsifiable assumption, not a prose complaint.

Do not let ZYAL optimize for pretty artifacts. It should optimize for rejected bad ideas with useful receipts. A healthy run has many vetoed candidates, clear reasons, and reproducible failure slices. Add a “red-team replay” mode that asks the physics adversary to construct a plausible but invalid candidate: a diagonal-likelihood winner, a hidden-prior winner, a parameter-fit masquerading as derived theory, and a null-test-credit inflation candidate. The host should reject each for the expected reason.

## 6. Software, reproducibility, and release discipline

The artifact boundary should be brutally simple. A review run receives a source tarball, extracts it into an isolated directory, verifies selected paths, computes a manifest, runs allowed commands, and emits only declared artifacts. It must never commit secrets, runtime state, real prompts, browser profiles, downloaded archives, logs, receipts, local override config, or private paths. Receipts are produced for audit, but unless the output contract asks for them, they are not included in the patch tarball.

For OpenQG core, add a `RunReceipt` shared type: `source_tar_sha256`, `git_commit`, `selected_paths`, `data_lock_hashes`, `command`, `env_redactions`, `random_seed`, `model_family`, `forward_fidelity`, `likelihood_mode`, `vetoes`, `scores`, and `artifact_hashes`. For every generated report, require regeneration commands and refuse hand edits. For every fixture, include a source lock and a semantic version; no candidate may silently use a newer public release.

Testing should include physics and systems invariants. Physics invariants: LCDM reproduces known compressed background fixtures within tolerance; GR null-test anchors remain zero without earning frontier credit; EDE/coupled-DE candidates are refused without the required fidelity; covariance changes affect likelihood; row order does not affect score. Systems invariants: tar traversal is impossible; selected-path allowlist is enforced; source receipts are deterministic; dashboard code cannot alter Rust policy; fake shell/browser/remote boundaries remain fakeable.

The mapped proof lane must remain runnable. Add small fixtures for every scary edge case rather than relying on production-scale data. A serious physicist does not need a thousand-agent demo; they need one reproducible chain from theory assumption to observable vector to likelihood to model comparison to veto receipt.

## 7. Acceptance criteria for the next credible milestone

A milestone worthy of external physicist attention should demonstrate exactly this:

1. The source archive is ingested with manifest and selected-path proof.
2. LCDM, wCDM, w0waCDM, and one running-vacuum/coupled-dark-sector candidate run on a covariance-aware distance-plus-growth fixture.
3. The report separates χ², AIC, BIC, evidence approximation, predictive holdout, strict-theory credit, and vetoes.
4. A deliberately overfit candidate is rejected for parameter leakage or complexity.
5. A background-only candidate is prevented from making perturbation or CMB claims.
6. A null-test-only unification candidate passes sanity checks but does not win frontier value.
7. ZYAL emits failure slices with root-cause labels, evidence refs, penalties, lineage, and deterministic route-policy normalization.
8. The final artifact can be reproduced by a clean checkout without private paths or runtime leftovers.

If the team ships that, the project becomes interesting: not because it has found new physics, but because it has made it harder for an automated system to fool itself while searching where new physics could actually show up.
