## 1. What the brief is really enforcing

The uploaded brief is an anti-leakage contract. It says, in effect:

A row may be `predicted` only if the number follows from a documented derivation that does **not** use the target fixture value, validation rows, benchmark feedback, reference predictors, fitted coefficients, copied constants, or empirical surrogates.

That means the right theory must separate four categories:

| Category                                     | Meaning                                                                      | Allowed output                                                 |
| -------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Universal law rows**                       | Values fixed by exact symmetry or limiting theory                            | `predicted`                                                    |
| **Structural derivations**                   | Gauge group, anomaly cancellation, charge quantization, chiral structure     | Useful dossier evidence, but not necessarily fixture rows      |
| **State-conditioned rows**                   | EHT ring size, GW event masses, galaxy rotation points, JPL orbital elements | `not_implemented` unless independent source-state inputs exist |
| **Unsolved constants / empirical summaries** | α, Yukawas, CKM, PMNS, ΛQCD, vacuum energy, dark matter abundance            | `not_implemented` unless actually derived                      |

The key insight across the best tips is the **law/state split**. A theory may derive GR’s equations, but that does not mean it derives the mass and spin of a particular GWOSC binary. A theory may derive the Kerr shadow formula, but that does not mean it derives M87*’s mass, distance, spin, plasma emission state, and imaging pipeline. The EHT M87* result, for example, measured a specific crescent diameter of about (42\pm3) microarcseconds for a specific astrophysical source, not a universal constant. ([arXiv][2])

## 4. Where the archive is weak

The repeated weakness is **score inflation**. Several tips score 86–88 while admitting that almost all numeric constants, particle masses, flavor observables, cosmological parameters, galaxy dynamics, atomic spectra, black-hole source quantities, and solar-system ephemerides remain unsupported.

The most common mistake is treating this:

[
\text{“I can write the governing equation.”}
]

as equivalent to this:

[
\text{“I can derive the fixture row value without using the fixture.”}
]

Those are not equivalent.

Examples:

| Domain               | Why broad equations are not enough                                                                                                                                                                  |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| CODATA constants     | Listing CODATA or deriving unit conventions does not derive (\alpha), mass ratios, or (G). NIST gives recommended values; that is not a first-principles derivation. ([NIST][4])                    |
| PDG particle rows    | PDG compiles measured/evaluated particle properties; a theory must derive masses, widths, lifetimes, and couplings independently. ([Particle Data Group][5])                                        |
| NuFIT neutrino rows  | NuFIT is a global empirical analysis of oscillation data; writing PMNS formalism does not derive the actual angles, splittings, ordering, or CP phase. ([arXiv][6])                                 |
| DESI / BAO rows      | DESI BAO distances are empirical cosmological measurements; deriving FLRW equations does not fix (H_0,\Omega_i,r_d,A_s,n_s). ([arXiv][7])                                                           |
| NIST ASD atomic rows | Atomic line/level values require constants, nuclear data, QED/QCD corrections, isotope assumptions, and many-body calculations. NIST ASD is an evaluated database, not a theory output. ([NIST][8]) |
| JPL Horizons rows    | Horizons ephemerides depend on initial conditions and solar-system state data, not just Newton/GR equations. ([JPL Solar System Dynamics][9])                                                       |
| SPARC rows           | Galaxy rotation curves need baryonic profiles, mass-to-light assumptions, and a dark-matter/modified-gravity closure; SPARC is a database of specific galaxies. ([Astroweb][10])                    |

## 5. What all candidates are still missing

The missing pieces are not cosmetic. They are the hard core of the problem:

1. **No derivation of (\alpha)**
   The fine-structure constant is the cleanest dimensionless target. None of the files derives it.

2. **No solved Yukawa/flavor sector**
   Modular flavor, entropy selection, finite spectral data, and fixed-point ideas are promising, but none supplies a unique, audited derivation of (Y_u,Y_d,Y_e,Y_\nu), CKM, PMNS, or CP phases.

3. **No real derivation of three generations**
   Several candidates gesture at index/rank/topological rules, but none proves exactly three replicated fermion generations from first principles.

4. **No solved electroweak hierarchy**
   (v/M_{\rm Pl}), (m_H), (\lambda_H), and top Yukawa remain unsupported or only formally constrained.

5. **No derivation of (\Lambda_{\rm QCD}), hadron masses, or (m_p/m_e)**
   Dimensional transmutation can be written, but the required UV coupling, scheme, thresholds, and hadron extraction are not derived.

6. **No vacuum-energy solution**
   The candidates do not explain the observed tiny cosmological constant without importing cosmological fits.

7. **No dark matter abundance or baryogenesis derivation**
   Axion/modulus/hidden-sector suggestions remain candidate mechanisms, not fixture-ready predictions.

8. **No source-state interface**
   EHT, GWOSC, SPARC, JPL, Pantheon, NIST ASD, and many cosmology rows require independent state data. The archive recognizes this but does not fully formalize a state-provenance grammar.

9. **No adapter run**
   None of the tips actually implements and runs the OpenQG adapter lanes. Therefore all scores are dossier scores, not benchmark results.

## `asd_*`

Atomic spectra require derived constants plus QED, nuclear, isotope, hyperfine, and many-body machinery. NIST ASD is an evaluated spectroscopic database, not an automatic prediction surface. ([NIST][8])
