# Candidate Paper Card

**Title:** Reconstructing the metric in group field theory

**arXiv:** 2312.10016

**Date:** 2023-12-15 (v1), 2024-11-11 (v3)

**Authors:** Steffen Gielen, Lisa Mickel

**Source:** arXiv

**Claims:**
- GFT coupled to four massless scalar fields used to define relational coordinate system
- Conserved currents derived from symmetries under scalar field shifts
- Matter fields satisfy Klein-Gordon equation exactly at quantum level
- Effective spacetime metric extracted from conserved currents
- Effective Friedmann equation derived shows bounce and late-time limit equivalent to GR with single scalar field

**Evidence Strength:** high

**Tags:** gft, metric-reconstruction, scalar-fields, friedmann, bounce

**Summary:** Uses scalar fields as a relational reference frame to extract an effective spacetime metric from GFT, deriving a Friedmann equation with a bounce and GR late-time limit.
