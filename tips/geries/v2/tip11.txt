5. **No state-input protocol mature enough for catalog rows:** GWOSC, EHT, JPL Horizons, SPARC, Pantheon+, and many BAO rows require historical or object-specific state data.

6. **No executed adapter:** the best proposals have adapter plans, not an implemented and run artifact.

7. **Overgenerous self-scoring:** several proposals award near-full credit for “equations exist” even when the actual numerical derivation remains missing.

This matters because the spectral-action program really can geometrize much of SM+gravity structure, but it does not automatically derive all low-energy measured constants. The original spectral action gives the Standard Model action coupled to gravity and high-scale coupling relations, and later noncommutative-geometry work incorporates neutrino mixing/seesaw structure, but the hard phenomenology is still not completely solved. ([arXiv][1])

Asymptotic safety is a serious UV-completion route, especially for gravity plus matter, but it also does not by itself provide a closed derivation of all SM masses, cosmological initial conditions, or catalog-level astrophysical states. ([arXiv][2])
