## 1. What the problem is really asking

The brief is not asking for a beautiful unified-theory essay. It is asking for an **auditable prediction machine**.

A row can be marked `predicted` only if this chain is closed without target data:

[
\text{primitive law}
+\text{derived constants}
+\text{derived or independently certified state}
+\text{declared estimator/extraction map}
\Rightarrow
\text{row value, uncertainty, unit, metadata}.
]

The biggest mistake in the archive is treating this as merely a policy. It is a theorem-level obstruction.

Writing the Friedmann equations does not derive DESI, BOSS, Pantheon, or Planck rows. Writing the CKM definition does not derive CKM angles. Writing a QCD beta function does not derive (\Lambda_{\rm QCD}). Writing Kerr geometry does not derive an EHT angular diameter without (M,D,a,i) and emission-state inputs. Writing (m_f=y_f v/\sqrt2) does not derive (m_f).

The benchmark is testing **derivability**, not eloquence or coverage-by-formalism.

Only 1 and 2 can support strict `predicted` rows.
