# Asymptotic safety in Lorentzian quantum gravity

arXiv: [2310.20603](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.20603)

## Evidence

> "We provide the first evidence for a background-independent, non-trivial fixed point
> for quantum gravity in Lorentzian signature, in the Einstein-Hilbert truncation."

## Claim-level Evidence

- **Background Independence**: RG flow evaluated with state-independent universal terms only
- **Lorentzian Signature**: Novel Wetterich-type fRG equation directly in Lorentzian spacetimes with covariant formalism
- **Fixed Point Existence**: Non-trivial fixed point similar to Reuter-type found

## Primary Source Excerpts

"While the RG flow is state-dependent, it is possible to evaluate state and background independent contributions to the flow."

"In this Letter, we provide the first evidence for a background-independent, non-trivial fixed point for quantum gravity in Lorentzian signature"

## Coherence with OpenQG Mission

This paper shows asymptotic safety is compatible with Lorentzian signature and background independence—core structural requirements for a predictive quantum gravity framework.
