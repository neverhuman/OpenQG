# Datasets to admit next

The current admitted data are enough for hardening, not enough for a serious cosmology claim. V6 uses compressed Planck distance priors, DESI BAO covariance blocks, growth/RSD-style observables, and S8-like amplitudes. `crates/openqg-core/src/scoring/covariance.rs` is the right likelihood substrate: it supports block covariance and marginalizes missing predictions by sub-covariance. But the dataset surface is still too compressed and too close to the proposer prompts.

First priority: SNe with covariance. The forward model already computes distance modulus through `crates/openqg-core/src/cosmology/background.rs::distance_modulus`, and the observable grammar supports `mu@z` in `crates/openqg-core/src/cosmology/observables.rs`. The missing part is a real Pantheon+/DES-SN style covariance loader and nuisance treatment. Supernovae are critical because they test the late-time expansion history that background drift currently exploits. They also weaken overreliance on the Planck compressed anchor. V7 should add a `MultiBlockFixture` or sparse covariance representation for SNe, plus nuisance parameters for absolute magnitude or calibration. Do not treat each SN magnitude as an independent `observables.len()` mode.

Second priority: an out-of-fit-set prediction registry. The novelty system cannot become credible while witnesses are generated during or after fitting. Add a registry file with `observable_id`, measurement program, predicted value, baseline value, mechanism field, registration timestamp/run id, and blinded/unblinded status. `binding.rs::audit_novel_predictions_with_context` should accept a registry handle, not just `fit_observables`. A witness earns novelty only if it was registered before the data were admitted to the fit set and has an external measurement sigma. Fit-set witnesses should be recorded as explanatory, not scored.

Third priority: full-shape or spectra data through a Boltzmann backend. The archive has `ForwardKind::Boltzmann` in `crates/openqg-core/src/cosmology/forward.rs::ForwardManifest`, but no implementation. Compressed distance priors are not enough for modified gravity. An f(R), nDGP, or dark-scattering claim affects growth, lensing, ISW, and potentially CMB spectra in ways compressed background distances do not capture. V7 does not need a production-grade Cobaya pipeline for every proposal, but it needs a promotion lane that rescans finalists through CLASS/hi_class or a documented external backend.

Fourth priority: growth covariance and scale information. The current growth path computes `fsigma8@z` and, for derived MG families, evaluates a fixed reference `k = 0.1 h/Mpc` in `crates/openqg-core/src/cosmology/growth.rs::GROWTH_KREF_H_MPC`. Real RSD constraints are survey- and scale-dependent, with Alcock-Paczynski covariance and model assumptions. The fixed-k path is a useful tier-1 approximation, but it should not be the final judge for scale-dependent f(R). Add dataset records that include effective k, redshift bin, covariance, and model-assumption tags.

Fifth priority: local H0 and BBN should be treated as consistency blocks, not free target bait. `proposer_memory.rs::build_data_brief` can mark H0 as a largest tension and steer the model toward it. That is useful, but H0 proposals are notoriously easy to overfit by moving background coordinates. V7 should keep H0 in a heldout or stress suite unless the model has a pre-registered mechanism that affects early or late distances consistently.

Expected outcomes: adding SNe covariance will likely further punish arbitrary `w0/wa` drifts unless the model lands near known wCDM/w0waCDM degeneracies. Adding full CMB or Boltzmann distance-prior validation will likely kill remaining anchor-calibration exploits. Adding out-of-fit registration will drive novelty points down sharply; that is desirable. A serious engine should initially find fewer "interesting" candidates.

Specific engineering changes: add `crates/openqg-data/src/registry.rs` entries for SNe covariance manifests, not just scalar records. Extend `crates/openqg-core/src/scoring/covariance.rs` to support sparse covariance or named covariance loaders, because full SNe covariance will not be pleasant as dense JSON. Add `crates/openqg-core/src/theory/prediction_registry.rs` with a typed `PredictionRegistry` and integrate it into `score_with_observables`. Add a CLI command in `crates/openqg-bench/src/cli/data.rs` to validate covariance dimensions, positive definiteness, observable matching, and manifest hashes.

Acceptance tests: one test where a fit-set `fsigma8@0.51` witness receives zero novelty; one where a pre-registered `fsigma8@1.2` or weak-lensing observable receives novelty only after being held out; one where a model missing SNe nuisance handling cannot claim full coverage; and one where a covariance block with duplicate or unknown observable ids fails closed. The goal is not more data for its own sake. The goal is to make the reward surface less local, less prompt-visible, and less compressible into a few hand-tuned dials.
